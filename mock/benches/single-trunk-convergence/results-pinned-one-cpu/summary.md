# Summary tables

The engine these tables time has no fusing yet and no adaptive trunk, fiber and morsel work on the dataflow schedule, so they are a baseline to compare against once those exist, and no design call rests on them. The region the engine is meant for is the large one, read here as fibers of 1048576 records and up run frame after frame; the short band of 16 to 1024 records, four morsels and fewer, lies outside it (op, 13 November 2026, the kaski channel, `briefs/fixup/op-answers-20.md` and `op-answers-21.md`).

Generated by `single-trunk-convergence --summary` from the CSVs beside this file. Each cell is the median time of one call over every batch of every pass, then in brackets the lowest and the highest of the per-pass medians. Every pass runs an arm in a worker subprocess of its own, so the bracket is the spread between processes.
