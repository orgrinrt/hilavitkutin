The search for calls and jumps in the bodies, and its control.

Emitted code: x86-64 linux, AT&T syntax, toolchain nightly-2026-05-28,
profile `probe` (inherits release: opt-level 3, lto "fat", codegen-units 1),
on the tree with the read product on the carrier.

command, from the sketch root:
cargo rustc -q -p probe --profile probe --lib -- --emit asm,llvm-ir
kept as `probe.s` and `probe.ll`.

An indirect call or jump in this syntax is `call` or `jmp` with a `*` operand.

pattern, any call or unconditional jmp: ^\s*(call|jmp)q?\s

probe.s, by symbol
  probe_control_indirect  line 10   jmpq *%rax     (the control: found)
  probe_native_drift      no line
  probe_native_warm       no line
  probe_value_drift       no line
  probe_value_warm        no line

The pattern does not look for conditional jumps. Each of the four bodies holds
two: the `je` that skips an empty window and the `jne` that closes the loop.
Between them a body is loads, an add and a store, and it ends in `retq`. The
value bodies hold no call to `apply`: the value function is inlined into the
loop.

probe_value_drift (lines 81 to 101) and probe_native_drift (lines 21 to 41)
are the same instructions, line for line.

probe.ll
  The only `call` instructions in the four bodies are to
  `llvm.experimental.noalias.scope.decl`.
