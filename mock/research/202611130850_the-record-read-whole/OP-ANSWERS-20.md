# Op answers 20, 13 November about 12:20 UTC: what hilavitkutin is for, and its benches

Op, verbatim, on the convergence bench's short-fiber result (below one to four morsels no parallel arm beats the sequential frame):

"Yeah hilavitkutin is intended for the cases that have millions of simulation and crunching work data going constantly. So 1-4 morsels is almost never a realistic use case for hilavitkutin in the first place
I think our benches should perhaps better reflect that"

Intent, in coord's words:

- Hilavitkutin is for workloads with millions of records of simulation and crunching work going constantly. That is the region its design and its benches answer to.
- A workload of one to four morsels is almost never a realistic case for it, so a result there is a boundary note, never a verdict on the engine.
- The benches reflect the intended region: their workloads, their headline findings and what is drawn from them are stated for the large, constant case first.

Kept for provenance, not an intent: "perhaps".
