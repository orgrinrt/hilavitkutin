# src changelist

Round topic `202611140543_topic.panic-aborts-on-a-worker.md`; the doc changelist `202611140546_changelist.doc.lock.md` states the design. The flat topic `202607200100_topic.b1-cap-lifting-completion.md` needs nothing from this round, as the doc changelist says. The boundary is the answer of the question ticket `a_unit_panic_aborts_behind_a_no_unwind_boundary_in_hilavitku` under its provisional stamp, not a ratified one.

## Current state

`crates/hilavitkutin/src/dispatch/wu_fn.rs:37-44`, `invoke_wu_in_fiber`, calls `wu.execute(ctx)` directly; it is the only call of `execute` in `crates/hilavitkutin/src`. `crates/hilavitkutin/src/scheduler/worker.rs:60`, `worker_main`, is a Rust-ABI function. A unit that panics on a `run_parallel` worker ends the executor's thread and the frame never returns; under `run` it unwinds into the caller.

## Changes proposed

### 1. The unit body runs behind a no-unwind boundary

`invoke_wu_in_fiber` calls `execute` through a new `#[inline(always)] extern "C" fn execute_without_unwinding<'frame, W>`, generic over the unit as the shim is, with a doc comment saying why. A panic in any unit body aborts at that edge, under `run`, `run_fused` and the workers of `run_parallel`.

### 2. The worker entry cannot unwind

`worker_main` becomes `pub(super) extern "C" fn`, with a doc paragraph saying it stops an unwind out of the worker's own code between units. The spawn site at `scheduler/run_parallel.rs:194-201` calls it unchanged.

### 3. The check

`crates/hilavitkutin/tests/unit_panic_aborts.rs`, unix only. A frame of two column-disjoint producers in the first phase and a combiner reading both in the second; the second producer panics, and its trunk is core 1's under `FixedWorkers::<2>` (trunks are owned round-robin by rank within the phase, `tests/gate2_run_parallel.rs:9-11`). The parent re-runs the test binary as a child through the entry it names and holds the child to dying by SIGABRT, no exit code, inside a 30 s bound, killing it past the bound.

`a_unit_panic_on_a_worker_aborts_within_the_bound` runs the child through `run_parallel`; `a_unit_panic_under_run_aborts` through `run`.

The cases that must fail, each shown in `mock/research/202611140543_panic-aborts-on-a-worker/`:

1. `unit-panic-before.txt`: on the tree before this changelist, the `run_parallel` child hangs and the bound trips at 30 s.
2. `unit-panic-worker-entry-only.txt`: with the shim's boundary taken out and the worker entry kept, the `run_parallel` case still aborts (the entry arm holds alone), and the `run` case fails: the child unwinds and exits 101 (wait status 25856), since no worker stands between the body and the caller.
3. `unit-panic-after.txt`: both boundaries in, all three green, 0.10 s.
