## Changes proposed

1. `hilavitkutin-providers/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, the `StaticBumpMemory` section: the type gains `pub const unsafe fn from_raw_parts(base: *mut MaybeUninit<u8>, len: USize) -> Self`, whose contract is the caller's: the `len` bytes at `base` are valid for reads and writes, nothing but the provider reads or writes them, and they stay so until the provider is dropped. `new` is the safe case over the borrowed slice. The argument for the trait's promises is restated over both constructors: the buffer outlives the provider, forever under `new` and by the caller's promise under `from_raw_parts`, which is what promise 2 asks.
