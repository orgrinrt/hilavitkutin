# Src changelist: the catalogue ledger and the sealed traits

**Date:** 2026-11-12
**Phase:** SRC
**Round:** 202611122316 (the-catalogue-ledger-and-the-sealed-traits, branch `work/dev-gate-green`)

Clears the thirty findings that keep `cargo mock` red on `dev`: twenty-two of `catalogue-ignore` and eight of `single-source`. No behaviour changes, no lint severity moves and no exemption is added. The ledger `mock/catalogue.md` and the `ledgers` key under `[lints.catalogue-ignore]` in `mockspace.toml` landed with the topic.

## CHANGE: every `#[ignore]` names the ledger row of its ticket
Files: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin/tests/adapt_perf_contracts.rs`, `a1_fiber_morsel_size.rs`, `gate2_worker_count.rs`, `r6_morsel_window_formula.rs`, `resource_snapshot.rs`; `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-persistence/tests/cold_store.rs`; `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-extensions-macros/tests/lto_smoke.rs`; `mock/benches/engine_vs_std/tests/perf_gate.rs`.
Each ignore reason becomes `catalogue: <the gap>; tracked #<id>`, where `<id>` is a row of `mock/catalogue.md`. The `#341` and `#344` ids, the `tracked: BACKLOG ...` and `tracked: FIXME ...` forms and the unprefixed `perf oracle` reasons are all replaced, and the `tracked ...` text in the bodies of the `unimplemented!` messages of the same tests follows the attribute.
Verification: `grep -rnE "tracked #(341|344)|tracked: (BACKLOG|FIXME)" mock/crates/*/tests mock/benches --include=*.rs` returns nothing; `cargo mock` reports no `catalogue-ignore` finding. Two doc comments in `src/` keep a legacy id and are left as they are (see Out of scope).

## CHANGE: the sealing traits of `hilavitkutin-api` and `hilavitkutin-linking` are crate-private
Files: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src/adapt.rs`, `builder.rs`, `dispatch_codegen.rs`, `hint.rs`, `meta.rs`, `run_cfg.rs`, `store_values.rs`; `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-linking/src/symbol.rs`.
Each `pub trait Sealed {}` in a private module becomes `pub(crate) trait Sealed {}`, the form `hilavitkutin-api/src/lib.rs` already uses, and every public trait that bounds on one carries `#[allow(private_bounds)]`, as `platform.rs`, `access.rs` and the `Depth` trait in `builder.rs` already do. The seal stays unnameable outside its crate. `hilavitkutin/src/resource/bindings/mod.rs` keeps its `pub trait Sealed`, which is then the only one the lint compares.
Verification: `grep -rn "pub trait Sealed" mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src mock/crates/hilavitkutin-linking/src` returns nothing; `grep -rn "pub(crate) trait Sealed" mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src mock/crates/hilavitkutin-linking/src` returns nine matches, the eight above and the `lib.rs` one; `cargo mock` reports no `single-source` finding.

## Tests

No test is added or changed in what it asserts: the ignored tests stay ignored, with the reason they already carried and a ticket that resolves. The sealing change is covered by the existing compile of every crate that bounds on these traits, since a consumer that could name a seal before would fail to build now.
