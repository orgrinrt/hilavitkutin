# the-catalogue-ledger-and-the-sealed-traits

Dev's gate is red on thirty findings of two lints, and the seat check of every job in this repository stops on them. This topic records what each finding is, what the repair is, and why neither repair lowers a gate or adds an exemption.

## Fifteen ignored tests tracked by ids that no longer resolve

The `catalogue-ignore` lint reads every `#[ignore]` and wants the reason in one shape, `catalogue: <the gap>; tracked #<id>`, where the id resolves to a row of a named ledger or to a row of the project's registry. The tests in `adapt_perf_contracts.rs`, `a1_fiber_morsel_size.rs`, `r6_morsel_window_formula.rs` and `resource_snapshot.rs` carry `#341` and `#344`, ids of a tracker that no longer exists. Those in `cold_store.rs` and `gate2_worker_count.rs` carry `tracked: BACKLOG ...` or `tracked: FIXME ...` instead of an id, and `lto_smoke.rs` carries the same. The seven perf oracles in `benches/engine_vs_std/tests/perf_gate.rs` carry a reason with no `catalogue:` prefix at all.

The repair is a ticket per distinct reason, filed with `--repos hilavitkutin`, and a new `mock/catalogue.md` holding one row per ticket id. The lint's `ledgers` key under `[lints.catalogue-ignore]` in `mockspace.toml` names that file, and each ignore cites its ticket's id. Fourteen tickets cover the twenty-two ignores. The seven oracles share one ticket, since they share one reason: a timing assertion means something only in a release build, and the bench harness is where it belongs.

No severity moves and no exemption is added. The key added to `mockspace.toml` names a ledger and changes nothing about how the lint gates.

## Eight sealing traits compared across crates

The `single-source` lint compares every plain `pub` type across crates and refuses the same name with the same shape defined twice. Eight `pub trait Sealed {}` sit in private modules of `hilavitkutin-api` and `hilavitkutin-linking`, and one sits in `hilavitkutin`. The lint reads the item's own qualifier, so a plain `pub` inside a private module is compared as `pub`, while a `pub(crate)` item is a crate's own and is not compared.

The repair makes the eight `pub(crate)`, the form `hilavitkutin-api/src/lib.rs` already uses for its crate-level seal and that `platform.rs` and `access.rs` already bound public traits on, with `#[allow(private_bounds)]` on each public trait that carries the bound. The seal stays unreachable from outside the crate, which is its whole job, and the one `Sealed` left in `hilavitkutin` stays the only one compared.

## Not touched

The design documents under `mock/crates/*/DESIGN.md.tmpl` describe the sealing idiom with `mod private { pub trait Sealed {} }` in one place, as an illustration and not as a declaration of a type, so they stay as they are. No crate API changes: nothing outside a crate could name these traits before, and nothing can now.
