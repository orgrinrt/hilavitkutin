# recover-landing-source

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** the source, tests, design templates, bench variants and `bench.toml` that the remote branch `recover/unlanded-rounds-and-benches` (tip `b24f158`, 30 August) carries and `dev` does not. Its record half (rounds, research, bench results) is on `dev` since `2f06ffe` and `6524900`.
**Source:** the coordinator's brief `job-hv-recover-landing`, part two.

The record on `dev` holds closed rounds whose source never reached `dev`. Their changelists are locked and filed, the benches that read their effect are filed, and the code they describe sits on a branch nobody merged. So `dev` describes one engine and ships another, and work done from `dev` since September reasoned from the older one. The clearest case is the swap: round `202611122301` left `replace_value` discarding its argument because round `202607193100` had removed the install, while round `202607200900`, closed a day after that one and filed on `dev`, puts the install back as the specified mechanism and its source changelist says so line by line.

This round lands that source. It decides nothing new. Each change below is the source or the design text of a round that is already locked, carried onto `dev`'s present shape.

## What the branch is

Thirteen commits on base `313d427`. Its last commits of 30 August copied files in from older branches that are gone from the remote, a whole file at a time. That has two effects this round has to work around. A file the branch alone edited can be an older text laid over a newer one, so no file is taken whole without comparing it to the base. And a file an older branch had deleted is still present on the recovery branch with only its `mod` line gone: `thread/pool.rs` and `dispatch/phase_run.rs` are there as orphans. The deletion is read from the locked changelist, not from the branch's tree.

## Appended after the review: the swap is not a settled thing being carried

The sentence above that this round "decides nothing new" does not hold for the swap, and a reviewer reading the branch with no part in the work said so. The implementing round `202607200900` is locked, and its own topic says the maintainer held ratification until the specification was implemented and benched, with the evidence returning to him for the call. `mock/research/seed/governance.md` still lists S1 through S7 as awaiting that ratification, and no ratifying words are recorded. A round on `dev`, `202611122301`, later kept `replace_value` discarding its argument on the strength of the July revert alone.

So two readings stand. One is that the hold meant implement and bench first and rule after, which makes carrying the implementation onto `dev` the precondition he asked for. The other is that nothing installs until he rules. The record does not choose between them, and the round does not either. The source carries the implementation, marked at the two methods as not final, and the call is question ticket `4b54e5a34009`, held by the maintainer, with the option of holding the swap out of `dev` named in it.

## Appended after the second review: the swap is held out of this landing

A second reviewer, in a fresh worktree and with no part in the work or in the first review, reached the same reading as the first. The strongest text on record is the maintainer's "revert it now" of 19 July. The sentence that he held ratification until the specification was implemented and benched is a locked round's paraphrase, the specification is still listed as awaiting him, and `dev`'s round `202611122301` kept the discard after that. Landing the install with a marker on it settles the open question by merging it, and a marker is not a hold.

Two readers agreeing is what this workspace asks before a call of this kind is acted on, so the round follows them. The swap is taken out of this branch whole: the install in `replace_value` and `replace_resource`, the plan-dirty bits and the published plan-band decision, the open `PlanAffecting` marker and its exclusivity with `Replaceable`, the swap tests and the swap bench variants, and the swap text in the design templates. On all of those this branch reads as `dev` does, the discard and its marker `FIXME(147e3897011f)` included. The source changelist that claimed the install is deprecated and a new one is written without it.

The opening of `PlanAffecting` is recorded as a ruling of its own, A2-3 of 19 July (`mock/research/seed/governance.md`, item 6). It is held with the rest all the same, because `dev`'s swap methods take the store marker and an open marker on value types has nothing to attach to until the methods take the value.

Nothing is dropped. The slice sits on `work/recover-landing-swap`, one commit range on top of this branch, with the reviewers' findings on it addressed there, and lands or changes on the answer to `4b54e5a34009`.
