## CHANGE: a swap installs the value
Files: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin/src/scheduler/dirty.rs`, `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src/store.rs`, `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src/run_cfg.rs`, `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src/lib.rs`.
From round `202607200900`, clauses S1 and S2. `replace_value` and `replace_resource` take a `PtrIndex` witness, find the binding through `Selector<T, PtrIndex>`, write the whole value into the slot the drain filled and mark `Resource<T>` dirty. `replace_resource` also sets the plan-dirty bit for the store. `PlanAffecting` is an open marker with no supertraits, and `impl<T: PlanAffecting> !Replaceable for T {}` makes the two routes exclusive, under `negative_impls`. `dev` had both methods discard their argument behind `FIXME(147e3897011f)`, reasoning from round `202607193100`, which round `202607200900` replaced. Both markers are gone.

The specification those clauses come from is implemented and benched and has no ratification on record: `mock/research/seed/governance.md` says it awaits it. Whether it is ratified, and whether its implementation may sit on `dev` before that, is question ticket `4b54e5a34009`, held by the maintainer. The two methods carry `FIXME(4b54e5a34009)` saying their shape is not final, and the install's safety comments carry `FIXME(74e2b9918149)` for the parked worker that keeps a shared reference across the swap under `run_parallel`.

## CHANGE: the plan band is entered on the first frame or on a plan-dirty bit
Files: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin/src/scheduler/mod.rs`, `builder.rs`, `defaults.rs`, `run.rs`, `run_fused.rs`, `run_parallel.rs`, `worker.rs`.

The scheduler carries `plan_band: AtomicBool`. `run` and `run_parallel` consume the plan-dirty bits at the top of a frame, publish the decision to `plan_band`, and the leading band and each worker read it. `__plan_band` is the hidden accessor the tests read. `dev`'s split of the scheduler into `run.rs`, `run_fused.rs`, `run_parallel.rs` and `worker.rs`, its `wanted` cells and its overflow verdict are kept as they are; the branch held the same logic in one file.
