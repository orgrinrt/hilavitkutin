# Doc changelist: the deallocate contract gains its alignment

**Date:** 2026-07-19
**Phase:** DOC
**Topic:** `202607192000_topic.reachable-bodies-contradicting-contracts.md`

The topic left one question open: where the original alignment comes from at the free site.
This changelist settles it, because the answer changes a published contract and so has to land
in the design docs before any body is edited.

## What the trace found

`MemoryProviderApi::deallocate(ptr, len)` does not carry the alignment, and its safety comment
requires only that `ptr` and `len` match a prior `allocate`. Alignment is simply absent from
the contract, so an implementation over `std::alloc` has nothing to reconstruct the `Layout`
from and guesses a word.

That guess is wrong for the engine's own allocations. `ArenaColumnStorage`
(`hilavitkutin-providers/src/storage.rs:99`) allocates every column slab at `CACHE_LINE_ALIGN`,
64 bytes, matching the canon requirement at consolidation spec `:562`. It frees them at `:78`
and at `:159`, the latter from `Drop`. Under `StdMemoryProvider` that is a free with a
mismatched layout, which is undefined behaviour.

The defect is also already known. `hilavitkutin-providers/tests/column_storage.rs:11` says in
its own module doc that `StdMemoryProvider` "is not used here because its `deallocate`
reconstructs the `Layout` with word alignment, which mismatches a 64-byte-aligned block under
`std::alloc`". The test picks the provider that cannot expose the bug. That is worth naming
plainly: a test written to avoid the failing path documents the failure without reporting it,
and the suite stays green while the contract stays broken.

## The decision: `deallocate` takes `align`

Three shapes were available. Store the alignment in a header beside each allocation, which
costs memory on every block and is wrong for a bump or arena provider that has no per-block
header to put it in. Have each provider remember its own allocations, which makes a stateless
provider stateful for no reason. Or pass the alignment back in, which is what `std::alloc`
itself requires: `dealloc` takes the `Layout`, not just the pointer.

The third is correct, and the reason is that our contract was the outlier. Every caller already
knows the alignment it asked for: both `ArenaColumnStorage` free sites have `CACHE_LINE_ALIGN`
in scope as a constant. The information was never missing, only dropped on the way down. Adding
it to the signature aligns our contract with the allocator underneath rather than inventing a
mechanism to recover what the caller could have said.

## CHANGE: `hilavitkutin-api/DESIGN.md.tmpl` deallocate signature and safety contract

`MemoryProviderApi::deallocate` is documented as taking `align` alongside `ptr` and `len`, and
the safety contract states that all three must match the values from the prior `allocate`. The
prose names why: an implementation over a layout-taking allocator cannot free correctly without
it, and a provider that ignores alignment (a bump arena) may simply not read the parameter.

## CHANGE: `hilavitkutin-providers/DESIGN.md.tmpl` arena free sites

`ArenaColumnStorage` is documented as passing `CACHE_LINE_ALIGN` at both free sites, matching
what it passes at reserve time. The 64-byte alignment is stated as the canon requirement it
serves rather than as an arbitrary constant.

## Not in scope

`protect` keeps its current shape. The `no_os` tier's `spawn` constraint is real and stays.
Nothing here changes what `allocate` does.
