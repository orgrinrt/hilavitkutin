# src changelist

Round topic `202611131745_topic.what-the-review-of-the-build-configuration-found.md`, doc changelist `202611131745_changelist.doc.lock.md`.

## mock/crates/hilavitkutin-build/src/pragma.rs

- `PragmaSet::coherent`, a `const fn`: MathPeephole goes when FastMath is out, Profiling when Pgo and Bolt both are.

## mock/crates/hilavitkutin-build/src/bootstrap.rs

- The `macro_rules!` that spliced the variable's name into two texts is gone. `RUSTFLAGS_VAR` is a `const`, its `lint:allow(no-bare-static-str)` naming `no_bare_static_str_does_not_know_a_build_dependency_only_cra`, and both texts are written with `write!`.
- `write_wrapper` writes `set -f` before `exec`.
- `configure_with` applies `coherent` after `tune`, reads `RUSTC_WORKSPACE_WRAPPER` through the closure, and writes a `cargo::warning` when `writes_flags` is true and no wrapper, or an empty one, is named. Its doc says what FastMath reaches.
- The `lint:allow(no-bare-string)` markers that sat alone on comment lines inside three bodies move onto the signature lines whose `&str` they are for.

## mock/crates/hilavitkutin-build/src/flags.rs

- `writes_flags(PragmaSet) -> bool`, through a sink that keeps only whether anything was written.
- `LOOP_OPTIMIZATION` carries the same `lint:allow(no-bare-static-str)` and ticket.

## mock/crates/hilavitkutin-build/src/requirements.rs

- A comment on the LoopOptimization row: the plugin is its plugin passes' requirement.

## mock/crates/hilavitkutin-build/src/lib.rs

- The crate docs name the two committed files and the workspace-member limit, and the FastMath row of the roster says the cfg is for the configured crate's own code.

## mock/.cargo/hilavitkutin-rustc-wrapper

- Rewritten from `write_wrapper`, with `set -f`.

## mock/crates/hilavitkutin-build/tests/bootstrap.rs

- `no_flag_is_one_the_pinned_toolchain_refuses` becomes `the_pinned_rustc_takes_every_release_flag`: the pinned `rustc` compiles a one-function library to an object under the release flags, and the control, `-Cllvm-args=-enable-unsafe-fp-math`, is refused by the same call.
- `configure_reads_profile_and_features_through_the_closure` becomes `configure_reads_profile_features_and_wrapper_through_the_closure` and asserts the literal directives.
- `a_missing_wrapper_is_warned_of_when_there_are_flags`: unset and empty wrapper warn, a set with no flag-writing pragma does not.
- `a_tuned_set_is_made_coherent`.
- `the_wrapper_appends_the_variable_and_nothing_else`: the committed wrapper with `echo` for rustc, the variable unset, empty, set, and `*`. Without `set -f` the last expands to the crate directory's files; that control was run and failed as it should.
- `the_committed_wrapper_is_the_written_one` checks the `set -f` line.
