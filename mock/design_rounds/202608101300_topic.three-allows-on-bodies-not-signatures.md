# Three allows, on bodies rather than signatures

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** `hilavitkutin-sym`

Round thirteen's free functions carry allows on their signatures, which hold. The
lint fires one line lower, on the body's cast, and on the two verdict predicates'
`bool` returns.

Nothing here is a design question. The uses are the two already-declared
exceptions: arvo's own `to_raw`/`from_raw` boundary, and a test-local predicate's
return type.

It is a round rather than a footnote for the same reason round ten was. Round
thirteen closed a real hole and took the gate from five to eleven, and a round
that leaves its own additions behind is the pattern this branch has criticised in
four separate rounds.

**The narrower lesson, which is new**: an allow on a function's signature does
not cover its body. Round thirteen assumed it did, having placed the comment on
the line the reasoning was about rather than the line the lint reads.
