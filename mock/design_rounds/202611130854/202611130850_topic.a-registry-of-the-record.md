The record that stands in for one is prose: the founding consolidation spec in round `202604200055`, the principles template, the seed under `mock/research/seed/`, and some 1500 further markdown files of rounds, research, sketches and imports. ## Changes proposed

1. The read. Every file of the record is read, and `LEDGER.md` under the research directory carries one line per file: its line count, its date, its standing by its own words, and what it holds. Files the recovery branch `recover/unlanded-rounds-and-benches` holds and `dev` does not are read from that branch and marked so.
2. The registry. `mockspace.toml` declares `canon_paths` over `mock/registry/` and three namespaces, `subject`, `tenet` and `question`. The first two take the shape kaski's registry has and the shared tool pack declares, so the canon tools read them unchanged when the pack is declared here. `tenet` gains four fields the transcription needs: `source` and `quote` for the passage a row rests on, cited by path and line and quoted exactly, `mechanism` for the technique or concrete shape a passage names, kept apart from the intent in `what`, and `origin` with `written_by` for whether the passage is op's own text or an agent-written proposal. `question` holds what the record leaves open or states two ways, with the passages and the options, and op's answers of 13 November with the options he chose among.
3. No row is ratified here. `standing` keeps the pack's closed set, and every row written in this round is `provisional`: a transcription of what a passage says, with nobody having walked it. A row never says more than its source passage does.
4. The answers. `ANSWERS.md` answers the eight questions of the brief from the record, with rows cited and passages quoted, and says where the record decides nothing.
5. The tools (`canon`, `already-said`, `unearth`) come from the shared pack once its upstream branch lands. Nothing is copied into this repository.

The choice of kinds, and what was weighed against arvo's `ruling`, `obligation`, `question` and `proposal`, is argued in the doc changelist.
