# Doc changelist: the promise stated as a law, and the derive that drops it

**Date:** 2026-08-09
**Phase:** DOC
**Topic:** `202608092200_topic.the-collision-is-still-reachable.md`

## CHANGE: the coverage section names the two laws that were missing

File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

The section lists what the tests must cover and does not name the crate's own
promise: that two origins never produce one handle. Six laws related declarations
to each other and to layout types, and a shape could still mint colliding handles
from two minters, because none of them minted anything.

Added: every origin's base and ceiling fit that shape's `ID_BITS`, and a
shape-generic disjointness law that mints from every origin of every shape.

The section also states the general form, which this crate has now met six times:
**a law over declarations is not a law over behaviour.** A width law cannot see a
collision. Only minting can.

## CHANGE: record that the trait hardcodes `Standard`'s id width

File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

`origin_base`, `origin_ceiling` and `id_from_counter` are typed `Uint<28, Hot>`
in the trait. Twenty-eight is the default shape's id width sitting at the one
place a shape cannot override, which is the same substance as the constant this
branch lifted, one level down and unlifted.

Documented as a known limit with the laws that contain it, rather than left for
a reader to discover from a signature.

## CHANGE: `Sym`'s equality bounds are the layout's, not the shape's

File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

The document says integer equality across the whole layout is the crate's central
promise. The derive puts `S: PartialEq + Eq + Hash + Default + Debug` on `Sym<S>`
while `SymShape` requires `Copy + 'static`, so a legal shape deriving less yields
a handle with no equality. The document states that the equality impls are bound
on the layout rather than on the shape, and why.
