# Topic: where R5 is light, and the amendment that removes the dissonance

**Date:** 2026-06-19
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Round:** 202606210600

The handle model (sibling topic `202606210600_topic.resource-handle-storage-model.md`)
is the canonical intent and is consistent with R5 read carefully. But R5 is **light**
(under-specified, not contradictory) on two points, and that lightness is exactly what
let the shipped impl drift to a one-record blob and let a reader conclude "resources are
a separate inline-value domain." Naming the gaps so the amendment can close them.

## Gap 1: per-member shape-bound decomposition is never stated outright

R5 says "Resources consist of three field types: `Field`/`Seq`/`Map`" (537, 1684). That
reads as the value's **composition/shape**, not as a **storage rule**. Nowhere does R5
say "each member is realized as its own column" or "those columns are **shape-bound**
(shared across resources by stride, not unique per concrete `T`)." The shape-bound point
is load-bearing: without it, type-unique-per-`T` columns explode the column count; with
it, `Field<u32>` everywhere collapses to one column. R5 is silent on this axis entirely.

## Gap 2: "separate arena" reads as resource-private, when only the handles are separate

R5 line 566 ("Separate arena for resource Seq/Map allocations") and 1686-1687 ("separate
arena") read as a resource-private value arena. The intent: the **values** live in the
**unified** type-erased columnar store; only the **handle store** (the pointers, keyed by
resource id) is separate, and that separation is what provides the pointer-provenance /
noalias win (1695-1704). The word "separate" is correct but attached to the wrong noun in
a casual reading. R5 line 1689 ("pointer indirection to external slab storage, NOT inline")
points at the right model, but the "separate arena" phrasing pulls against it.

## The amendment

A canonical addendum (`mock/research/202606210600_resource-storage-model-canonical-addendum.md`,
this round) states outright: (1) a `Resource<T>` is a handle; its `Field`/`Seq`/`Map`
members decompose into per-member **shape-bound** columns in the **unified** store; (2)
"separate" applies to the **handle store** and its pointer provenance, not to a
resource-private value arena; the values are unified columns; (3) `Seq`/`Map` = ptr+len
over consecutive type-erased strides. The addendum is the durable clarification so a
future reader (or agent) does not re-derive the drift. Whether the consolidation spec
text itself is patched, or the addendum is treated as the authoritative refinement, is an
op call captured during synthesis.

Note: this amendment assumes the handle model is the right design. The sibling pressure-
test topic (`202606210600_topic.storage-model-pressure-test.md`) questions exactly that
before the amendment is treated as locked.
