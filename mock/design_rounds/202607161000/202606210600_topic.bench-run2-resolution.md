## The V1-vs-V4 tiebreak lands in the near-identical branch

Per the tiebreak rule: V4 noticeably behind V1 chooses V1; near-identical chooses V4/hybrid because the
plugin-interop benefit is then free. The v4/v1 median ratio across all nine scalar arms is 0.991x to 1.017x
in run-2 (0.946x to 1.049x in run-1): every arm within +-1.7%, indistinguishable from 1.0x. V4 also ties
the blob on axes B, C, E (contiguous erased byte store, per-resource line). So V4 carries no measurable
latency cost against V1; its only real cost is the backcast machinery and the type-erasure soundness
burden, and against that it buys plugin-crossing capability the monomorphised blob structurally cannot
provide.

The bench has therefore removed the latency dimension from the V1-vs-V4 question. What remains is a pure
requirements question, op's to decide.

## Decided by the bench (independent of the requirements call)

- Resource is a handle, not an inline-value store (R5:1689).
- The per-record value layout is a one-record blob (per-resource contiguous on the arena), NOT decomposed
  per-member columns (V2: loses axis B and C) and NOT shape-bound shared columns (V3: loses axis E plus the
  resize-invalidates-all-sharers hazard). This reverses the addendum's original decompose-to-shape-bound.
- Scalar Field members: stack-snapshot before the morsel loop (wall-clock-neutral on M1, free insurance,
  mechanism proven in disasm, real where the reload is not L1-cheap).
- Seq/Map collection members: stream live from the column, never snapshot-copy (~2.5x at 64 MiB). The
  snapshot copies only the small scalars and the collection ptr+len, not the elements.
- The noalias invariant is architectural: the handle store never aliases the value columns, and scalars are
  snapshotted before the loop.

## The one open question for op

Is a plugin-crossing resource (a resource defined or accessed across a cdylib/wasm boundary) an actual
requirement for hilavitkutin's consumers (viola cdylib plugins, future clause extensions)?

- **Yes** -> adopt the hybrid: the erased static-shape boundary (V4/loimu shape) for exactly those
  resources, at zero measured latency penalty, plus the V1 blob for in-process resources. Sub-question then:
  is the erased boundary per-resource (only plugin-shared resources go erased) or global?
- **No** -> the pure V1 blob is the simpler default and the erased path is not built.

The bench established the choice is latency-free either way. It cannot decide the requirement.

## Drift-fix scope (bench-backed, additive)

The `DrainStores` one-record blob (`resource/bindings.rs`) is drift only in lacking the snapshot, not in
being a blob (the bench confirms the blob is the right per-record layout). The fix is additive: add the
scalar stack-snapshot before the morsel loop, wire live-streamed Seq/Map access, state the noalias
invariant. `CollectionBytes`/`ResourceFootprint` (#163/#164) supplies the collection-size term for the A3b
L1 morsel formula, which unblocks once this lands. If the hybrid is chosen, the erased boundary is a
separate additive path for plugin-crossing resources only.
