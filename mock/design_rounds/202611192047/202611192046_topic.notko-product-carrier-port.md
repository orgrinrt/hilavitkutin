# notko-product-carrier-port

**Date:** 2026-11-19
**Scope:** a defect fix, the three crates that name notko's product

notko `dev` at `15a95316` gives the product of a list its carrier as a parameter (`Project<C>`, with `Val`, `Col`, `ColMut` and `Arr<N>` as the carriers; notko `docs/design/notko-hlist-product-carrier.md`). hilavitkutin names notko by branch `dev`, and `mock/Cargo.lock` held it at `8a74dbcd`, where `Project` took no parameter and its product took the carrier as `Prod<C>`. Every build that resolves notko fresh fails: the generated bench driver and every bench arm (no bench of the tree runs on dev `67e63ca4`), and any consumer pinning a hilavitkutin rev (ticket `hilavitkutin_names_notko_by_branch_dev_so_a_consumer_pinning`).

stack-coord's call on 19 November, on the channel: "Neither: fix forward. It's a defect fix and stands alone. ... Port every site to notko 15a9531's Project<C>/Has<C,M,P>/SubOf<C,S,Ps> in a defect round on its own branch, run the touched crates' suites, and land it with land-own before B1b. No rev pin." The intent: hilavitkutin builds against notko's shipped product shape; the pin stays a branch.

The sites `cargo check` names after `cargo update -p notko -p notko-hlist`, and the grep for the old spelling: `hilavitkutin-api` `src/values.rs` (the `WriteSet` bound), `hilavitkutin-conformance` `src/oracle/walk.rs` (`<L as Project>::Prod<Val>`), and `hilavitkutin` `src/dispatch/engine_ctx/map.rs` (`<Self as Project>::Prod<Val>`). Each spelling moves to the carrier as a parameter of the trait: `Project<Val>` and `<L as Project<Val>>::Prod`. No design template names notko's product in the old spelling, so the doc changelist changes no template. Nothing changes in what any of the three crates means.

The read product stays hilavitkutin's own here: moving the read lists onto notko's product is the hila-engine track's job, and this round only keeps the existing shape building.
