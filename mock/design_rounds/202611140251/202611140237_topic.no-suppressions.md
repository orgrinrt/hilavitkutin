## The 24, by the lint each one silenced

### `private_bounds`, five sites

`hilavitkutin-api` `src/access.rs:80` on `HoldsAll`, `src/store.rs:225` on `BufferMode` and `:336` on `Starts`; `hilavitkutin-conformance` `src/oracle/keeping.rs:15` on `Keeping` and `src/oracle/visiting.rs:13` on `Visiting`; `hilavitkutin` `src/resource/bindings/column.rs:143` on `ColKind`.

The lint reads nominal visibility. A probe on the pinned nightly (a crate with one `pub trait` in a private module and one `pub(crate) trait`, each the supertrait of a `pub` trait) warns on the `pub(crate)` one only. The api's `sealed::Sealed` (`src/lib.rs:22`) and the conformance crate's (`src/oracle/mod.rs:28`) are `pub(crate)`, so the bound is reported. Declared `pub` inside their private modules they are as sealed as before, since no path outside the crate names the module, and the lint is satisfied. `access.rs`'s `seal::Walk` and `column.rs`'s `sealed::Sealed` are `pub` in a private module already, so their attributes were never needed.

### `dead_code` in shared test modules, five sites
