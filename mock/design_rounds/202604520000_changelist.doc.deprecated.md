### Add new section: "Access-set validation (compile-time)"

Insert after "Provider Kits". Section content:

- Frame the validation guarantee: at `.build()` time, the scheduler proves that every registered WorkUnit's `Ctx` bounds (the seven Has-traits) are satisfied by the registered providers + Resources/Columns + Kit-installed projections.
- Name the type-list construct (final naming TBD in SRC; placeholder: `AccessList`, `Demand`, `Supply`, `SatisfiedBy`).
- Sketch the rule: `.add::<WU>()` advances the `Demand` type-list by appending WU's access-set requirements; `.resource()` / `.column()` / `.install()` / platform setters advance the `Supply` type-list. `.build()` is gated on a sealed `SatisfiedBy<Supply>` impl for `Demand`.
- One paragraph on the user-facing experience: misconfigurations surface as trait-bound errors at the `.build()` call site. Custom error messages are out of scope (follow-up polish).
- One paragraph on the no-runtime-discovery principle: validation is monomorphisation-friendly, no `dyn`, no `TypeId`.

### Add new subsection: "Type-state accumulator"

Insert after the "Scheduler builder" section. Section content:

- Show the new builder type signature: `SchedulerBuilder<MU, MS, ML, D, S>` where `D` is the Demand type-list and `S` is the Supply type-list. Default values for `D` and `S` are the empty list at builder construction.
- Per-method advancement table:
  | Method | Advances | How |
  |---|---|---|
  | `.add::<WU>()` | `D` | Appends `WU::Read`, `WU::Write`, and the seven Ctx-bound projections |
  | `.resource::<T>(init)` | `S` | Appends a `Resource<T>` supply marker |
  | `.column::<T>()` | `S` | Appends a `Column<T>` supply marker |
  | `.memory(p)` | `S` | Appends a `HasMemoryProvider` supply marker |
  | `.threads(p)` | `S` | Appends a `HasThreadPool` supply marker |
  | `.clock(c)` | `S` | Appends a `HasClock` supply marker |
  | `.install::<K>(k)` | depends on Kit's installed methods | Kit calls the above in any order |
  | `.build()` | proves `D ⊆ S` | Compile error if missing |
- One paragraph on type-list mechanics (placeholder names; final in SRC): sealed marker traits + tuple-shaped concatenation; uses already-enabled features (`adt_const_params`, `generic_const_exprs`, `specialization`).
- One paragraph on backward compatibility: the new type-state defaults at construction time mean existing call sites that don't go through `.add::<WU>()` (like the current `tests/scheduler_builder.rs`) continue to compile so long as `.build()` doesn't fail the satisfaction check; for empty WU sets, `D` is empty and any `S` satisfies it.
