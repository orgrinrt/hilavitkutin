# A dead vocabulary term, introduced by the round that criticised not sweeping

**Date:** 2026-08-09
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** `hilavitkutin-sym`

Round seven's rustdoc uses `chain` for a chain of reasoning. In this repo
`chain` is dead vocabulary reserved to `fiber`, an ordered sequence of work
units, and the lint says so at `shape.rs:128`.

The lint is right about the word and wrong about the intent, which is the usual
shape of a vocabulary finding and is not a reason to allow past it. A reserved
term used for something else is exactly what the reservation exists to prevent,
because a later reader greps the term and finds a meaning nobody intended.

The fix is one word: `route`, which carries the same sense and is not reserved.

The round is opened for it rather than left, because round seven's own criticism
of round six was that it fixed the listed findings and did not sweep what was
outstanding. A violation this round introduced is the cheapest possible instance
of that, and skipping it because it is small is the same move at a smaller scale.
