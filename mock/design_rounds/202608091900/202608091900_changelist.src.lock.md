## CHANGE: `chain` becomes `route` in the `FieldWidth` rustdoc

File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/src/shape.rs:128`

`chain` is reserved to `fiber` in this repo's vocabulary and the strict gate
reports it. The sense here is a chain of reasoning, which is not what the
reservation covers, and using a reserved term for a second meaning is what the
reservation exists to prevent: a later reader greps it and finds a meaning
nobody intended.

`route` carries the same sense and is not reserved.

Verification: `mock --lint-only --strict --scope hilavitkutin-sym,hilavitkutin-str`
reports no `vocabulary-discipline` finding, run after the autofix and measured
rather than assumed.
