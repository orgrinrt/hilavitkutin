## Finding one: arvo's current dev does not carry what this design depends on

The design names the arvo crates the engine runs on, by name, twice. `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl:30` says the engine pulls `arvo`, `arvo-bitmask`, `arvo-graph`, `arvo-sparse`, `arvo-spectral` and `arvo-comb`. `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl:50` gives each plan step its crate: `arvo-bitmask` for set operations, `arvo-graph` for the DAG and RCM, `arvo-sparse` for the CSR dependency graph, `arvo-spectral` for the Fiedler partition, `arvo-comb` for the cost DP. `mock/crates/hilavitkutin/DESIGN.md.tmpl` repeats the list under its dependencies section. `mock/PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl:175-178` routes every `usize`, integer, float and bool through `arvo` types (`USize`, `UFixed`, `FastFloat`, `Bool`).

At `65c1634d` none of those crates exists. `git ls-tree origin/dev mock/crates/` in arvo lists four: `arvo-bits`, `arvo-format`, `arvo-placement`, `arvo-strategy`. A crate depending on `arvo` at that revision does not resolve:

```

location searched: Git repository ssh://git@github.com/orgrinrt/arvo.git?rev=65c1634d
```

The four that do exist are not the same surface under new names. `arvo-bits` at `65c1634d` is `Bits<const N: u32>(u64)` with `masked`, `raw` and `cast` and no arithmetic; the `Bits<N, S, Sign>` hilavitkutin uses is a strategy-parameterised numeral. `arvo-format` carries format descriptors (`points::UFixed<BITS, FRAC>` is a marker, not a value type) and a `Bool`. `arvo-strategy` carries `Hot`, `Cold`, `Warm`, `Precise` as presets of a new `Strategy` trait. There is no `USize`, no `Identity`, no mask, no graph, sparse, spectral or combinatorial routine, no tensor capacity type, no content hash and no refit.

hilavitkutin names 38 distinct arvo symbols across 188 files and 452 sites. The counts, from `grep -rhoE '\barvo(_[a-z]+)?::[A-Za-z_:]+' mock/crates`, are led by `arvo::USize` (155), `arvo::strategy::Identity` (49), `arvo::Bool` (20), `arvo_tensor::Dim` (15) and `arvo_tensor::Capacity` (14).

This is not a gap arvo forgot. arvo deleted the tree on purpose, in `929c46ed` and again in `0d3afdab`, and its `mockspace.toml` says not to restore it and not to reason from it. arvo's own canon records what hilavitkutin needs as obligations, each quoting this repo's design: `set_operations_over_a_fixed_size_bit_set`, `ordering_a_directed_acyclic_graph`, `a_sparse_adjacency_a_plan_can_be_built_on`, `a_spectral_partition_of_a_dependency_graph`, `a_cost_dynamic_program`, `a_platform_sized_unsigned_integer_at_an_api_position`, `an_exact_width_container_a_consumer_can_alias_and_pin`, `a_content_hash`, `a_build_flag_that_changes_float_semantics` and `a_primitive_for_every_position_a_bare_number_would_take`, all in `arvo/mock/registry/obligation.toml`. None has a delivering crate on `dev`.

So moving onto `65c1634d` is not a migration. It is one of two things, and neither is this round's to decide.

- hilavitkutin grows its own numeral, mask, graph, sparse, spectral, combinatorial and hash code. That puts arvo's obligations inside the consumer that stated them, which contradicts the design sentences above and the layering the forbidden-imports rules encode.
- hilavitkutin drops the design steps those crates serve, which is a redesign of the plan chain.

The call is handed back. Until arvo delivers those obligations, the last arvo `dev` revision carrying the surface this design names is `1b2796e9`, the first-parent commit immediately before `0d3afdab` deleted the tree. It is on `dev` and already carries `7b3b0909`.

## Finding two: the build at `1b2796e9`

The branch this round builds on pins every arvo dependency at `1b2796e9`. At that pin the workspace fails to compile because `7b3b0909` replaced `Identity::ZERO` and `Identity::ONE` with one constant per operation, `Identity<Additive>::IDENTITY` and `Identity<Multiplicative>::IDENTITY`, and took the inherent-looking `USize::ZERO` with it. The reasoning is in that commit: an identity belongs to its operation, and a purely fractional type has no multiplicative one. Re-exposing `ZERO` on the concrete types is the shape it rejected.

The migration is to name the operation at every site: `USize::ZERO` becomes `<USize as Identity<Additive>>::IDENTITY`, `USize::ONE` becomes `<USize as Identity<Multiplicative>>::IDENTITY`, and `<T as Identity>::ZERO` becomes `<T as Identity<Additive>>::IDENTITY`. hilavitkutin's own `ZERO` constants on its id types (`UnitId`, `FiberId`, `PhaseId`, `TrunkId`, `MorselBudget`) are its own surface and stay.

One fixture still pins arvo by branch: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-extensions-macros/tests/lto_smoke_fixture/Cargo.toml`. It moves to the same revision. The research sketches under `mock/research/sketches/` pin by branch as well; they are frozen spikes and are not touched.

The last three groups are design conflicts, taken one at a time below.

### The std tier

`mock/crates/hilavitkutin/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, Platform implementations, names three tiers, one of them a std fallback using `std::alloc`, `std::thread` and `std::time::Instant`. `mock/PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl` under no_std, no alloc says every crate is `#![no_std]` with no allocation, and names the std tier in its next sentence. The forbidden-imports rule for `hilavitkutin*` says the same without the carve-out. The source reconciles them with `extern crate std` inside a `#![no_std]` crate, which the workspace forbids outright: a crate that needs std does not claim `no_std`, and where it has two halves the halves are two crates. A second crate does not resolve it either, because the `hilavitkutin*` scope forbids std in every crate here. The design's tier has no home in this repository under its own rules. Handed back.

### hilavitkutin-build

`mock/crates/hilavitkutin-build/DESIGN.md.tmpl` has the crate read `CARGO_CFG_*`, emit `cargo:rustc-cfg` lines and write config files from a build script, which is std by nature. The crate already does not claim `no_std`. `mock/PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl` and the `hilavitkutin*` forbidden-imports scope say every crate is. The design of the crate and the principle over it disagree. Handed back.

### hilavitkutin-persistence

`mock/crates/hilavitkutin-persistence/DESIGN.md.tmpl` lists `hilavitkutin-str` as a dependency, the persistence sweep round `202604201319` built on it, and the design takes all memory from the shared `MemoryProvider`, whose trait lives in `hilavitkutin-api`. The forbidden-imports rule for the crate forbids both, and `mock/agent/rules/cargo.md.tmpl` says persistence and str do not depend on each other. The per-crate design and the lint config disagree. Handed back.

## Op's rulings on the three handed-back conflicts, 2026-09-18

Verbatim record: `.data/op-responses/202609181645_hila-std-tier-persistence.md` in the workspace repository.

The std tier. The option he chose: "hilavitkutin ships only the executor contract (a trait). The OS-thread executor lives in the consumer, or in a crate outside the hilavitkutin* family, gated per target and feature." His answer: "Consumer supplies it". The intent is that no `hilavitkutin*` crate spawns threads; `ThreadPoolApi` is the whole of what the engine owns. Applied to `std_tier.rs` and equally to `OsThreadPool` in `platform/os.rs`, which spawned pthreads under the same tier logic and falls under the same sentence. Tests and the `engine_vs_std` bench define their own executors in test and bench code.

Persistence. His answer: "Builds on str and api". The intent is that the per-crate design governs and the lint line was wrong; the `forbidden-imports` rule for `hilavitkutin-persistence` now forbids only `hilavitkutin` and `hilavitkutin-ctx`, and its reason says what the crate builds on.

hilavitkutin-build. Settled by the workspace rule that forbids `extern crate std`, not by a ruling: the build script owns the environment and the filesystem, and the crate is `#![no_std]`, takes the profile and target feature strings as parameters, and writes its directives and config file text into any `core::fmt::Write`. Nothing in the crate's design needed std for itself; each value it read from the environment is one a build script can pass in.
