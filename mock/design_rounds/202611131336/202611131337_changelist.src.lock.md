## CHANGE: `hilavitkutin/src/scheduler/dirty.rs`, the comment above the two swap methods

`FIXME(4b54e5a34009)` is replaced by a plain comment. The ticket it named asked the maintainer whether the swap specification may land, and it is answered: "Land as provisional". A FIXME is a marker for work still owed, and nothing is owed on that question any more, so the marker goes and the comment says what holds instead.

What the comment says: the two methods follow clauses S1, S2 and S4; the specification is implemented and benched and not ratified; they ship under the provisional stamp of 2026-11-13, so they may be called and a ratification can still change their shape. It then points at the two places the implementation and the specification differ, by the tickets that already mark them in the source: `FIXME(0ba7325f0216)` on the plan-dirty bit twelve lines below, and `FIXME(b8637199eb8a)` in `run_fused.rs`.

No code changes. The two `ptr::write` installs, the plan-dirty bit, `consume_plan_dirty`, `mark_dirty` and `dirty_units` are untouched, and so is every other file in the crate.

## NO CHANGE: the two gap markers and the aliasing marker

`FIXME(0ba7325f0216)` (the bit keyed by the store position where S2 says the plan-affecting id, and the capacity-zero case that drops it silently), `FIXME(b8637199eb8a)` (a fused frame that neither consumes nor honours the bit) and `FIXME(74e2b9918149)` (the parked worker holding a `&Scheduler` across the `&mut self`) all stay exactly as they are.
