# Src changelist: one definition of the accumulator split, and tests that reach what they name

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Phase:** IMPL
**Topic:** `202611131256_topic.one-slice-definition-and-tests-that-can-fail.md`
**Doc changelist:** `202611131259_changelist.doc.lock.md`

No behaviour of the engine changes. One function moves so that two callers share it, four tests are rewritten so that they can fail, three tests are added, and one constructor is `const` again.

## CHANGE: `accum_slice` is the one definition, and it lives with the bindings

`accum_per` and `accum_slice` leave `scheduler/worker.rs` and go to `resource/bindings/per_core.rs`, after `region_start`. `accum_slice` is `pub(crate)` and re-exported from `resource::bindings`; `accum_per` is private to the module. Both are `const fn` now, with the clipping written as comparisons the way `region_start` writes it.

The worker imports `accum_slice` from `resource::bindings` and calls it as before. The merge in `MergeAccums for AccumBinding` takes core c's slice start from `accum_slice(counts.total, counts.ncores, c)` where it had `(c * counts.per).min(counts.total)` written out. `PerCoreCounts` loses its `per` field and `PerCoreCounts::new` its first parameter. `scheduler/run_parallel.rs` no longer computes a stride or imports `accum_per`.

After this the expression that clips a slice start to the record count occurs once in the crate, in `accum_slice`. `grep -rn "per).min\|\* per" mock/crates/hilavitkutin/src` finds that one site.

The doc comment of `RebaseBindings` said a worker drives its dispatch over "its head+tail record slice". The slice is a region of the N-way tiling and has nothing to do with head+tail convergence, so the two words go.

## CHANGE: the alignment a free is given is read at the two sites that free

`tests/resource_bindings.rs` in the engine: `column_free_sees_the_alignment_its_reserve_used` is deleted. It called the test provider's `allocate` and `deallocate` itself and asserted the argument it had just passed. In its place `a_dropped_scheduler_frees_its_column_at_the_alignment_it_reserved` builds a scheduler with one resource on the counting provider, reads the alignment the provider was given at the reserve (64), drops the scheduler, and asserts one free at that same alignment. The counting provider gains `LAST_ALLOC_ALIGN` beside `LAST_DEALLOC_ALIGN`.

`hilavitkutin-providers/tests/column_storage.rs` gains a recording provider (a ledger of every block handed out and handed back, with address, length and alignment) and two tests on the store's own freeing sites. `a_second_reserve_frees_the_first_block_as_it_was_reserved` reserves one id twice and asserts the first block comes back exactly as it went out. `drop_frees_every_live_block_as_it_was_reserved` drops a store holding two columns and asserts the freed blocks are the reserved blocks.

Shown failing first: with the two `deallocate` calls in `hilavitkutin-providers/src/storage.rs` given `USize(8)` in place of `CACHE_LINE_ALIGN`, both store tests fail on the block comparison, and with the one in `Drop` alone the engine test fails with `left: 8, right: 64`. The store was put back and `git diff` on it is empty.

## CHANGE: the width laws are functions, and the fixtures are held to them

`hilavitkutin-sym/src/shape_tests.rs`: the three width laws were closures inside three tests. They are now three verdict functions, `widths_account_for_the_handle`, `id_is_origin_plus_counter` and `declared_widths_are_the_layouts`, with `satisfies_every_width_law` as their conjunction. The three shipped-shape tests keep their names and assert the functions over `Standard`, `SixteenMinters` and `WideKind`.

`the_collapsing_shape_still_satisfies_every_width_law` and `the_out_of_range_shape_still_satisfies_every_width_law` each added four literals from the fixture above them. They now assert `satisfies_every_width_law` on their fixture. That includes the law against the layout, which the fixture's own literals do not decide, so a fixture whose declared widths drift from its layout fails here.

A new fixture `WidthsItsLayoutLacks` declares a five-bit kind and a twenty-six-bit id over the standard layout, which carries three and twenty-eight. `the_layout_law_rejects_widths_the_layout_does_not_carry` asserts it passes the two arithmetic laws and is refused by the layout law. This is the case that must fail for the layout law, which had none.

The comment on `IndexPastOriginBits` no longer says which review found it.

## CHANGE: the fiber-window test asserts each count on its own unit

`tests/a4_parallel_fiber_windows.rs` sorted the observed and the expected morsel counts before comparing them. It now asserts the narrow column's unit ran `ceil(records / max(w0, w1))` times and the wide column's unit `ceil(records / min(w0, w1))` times. The header no longer speaks of a head+tail branch of the dispatch, which does not exist.

Shown failing first: with `run_core_phase` taking each fiber's window from the next descriptor, the test fails with `left: 3, right: 2` on the narrow unit. The sorted comparison passed that walk, since it produces the same two counts on the other units. The worker was put back.

## CHANGE: `Generator::single` is a `const fn`

`hilavitkutin-sym/src/generator.rs`. `dev` had `pub const fn Generator::new`; the constructor that replaces it had lost the `const`. Its doc comment names a test, `single_is_total_because_standard_has_one_origin`, that did not exist. `hilavitkutin-sym/tests/generator.rs` now has it: it asserts the default shape's one origin is in range, which is the refusal `single` skips, and holds a generator in a constant, which stops compiling if `single` stops being `const`.
