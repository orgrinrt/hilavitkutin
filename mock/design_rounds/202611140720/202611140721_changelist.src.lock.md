## Current state

`tests/run_parallel_wide_differential.rs` holds `run_parallel`'s counts to `run`'s trace through a factor per walk, on the unit-outer walk once on every core with a slice of the N-way tiling, which expects every non-commutative fiber split across the cores; a test asserts the tiling arithmetic against a copy of it. The record-less build has a stores test that compares empty with empty, and its count test passes because `run` and `run_parallel` both run everything. The only broken-runner control alters the sink. `skip_wide`'s `Out<K>`, `Sink` and `Seed` are read and written through `u32` pointers with no layout stated.

The provider and panic controls of the first pass are prose in `controls.txt`. `from_raw_parts` has no test of its own.

## Changes proposed

### 1. The counts are judged against the cone and the canon

`build_checks!` loses its per-walk factor. `run`'s cone check and `run_parallel`'s count check share one judge, `against_the_cone`: a unit in the cone of a step runs as often as `run` ran it in the cold frame, a unit outside it not at all, and the units the build names unjudged are held to nothing (the accumulator writer, and on the unit-outer build the producer sharing its fiber). The engine is one at every core count (row `the_engine_is_one_and_the_core_count_is_configuration`) and no fiber of the fixture declares itself commutative, so none is split (row `ordering_is_guaranteed_by_construction`, the spec `:604-607`). `tiled_cores` and `the_unit_outer_tiling_counts_the_cores_with_a_slice` are deleted.

The unit-outer count is red at frame 0 on producer 0 at every count, and is ignored under the new catalogue row `the_ungated_n_way_record_split_ships_where_its_own_round_del` (ticket 116), its reason naming the one frame body (115) and the skip (157) beside it. The record-less count is red at frame 1, ignored under `run_parallel_dispatches_every_unit_each_pass_and_never_asks`. The windowed count stays red under that row, at frame 1.

### 2. The record-less build is judged by what it can show

Its stores test is deleted; it keeps the cone test (red, `hilavitkutin_run_skips_nothing_on_a_record_less_frame`) and the count test (red, as above).

### 3. A control for the accumulator comparison

`traces` and `stores_check` take the frame and an `Alter`, `Sink` or `LastKept`, applied by `alter!` to the skipping run after that frame. `the_comparison_reports_a_runner_that_dropped_one_accumulator_record` drops the last live record of the unit-outer build's accumulator after frame 3 and asserts the comparison reports `Bytes { frame: 3, column: ROOTS + 1, record: kept - 1 }` at every count, the length branch of `first_bytes_difference`.

### 4. The fixture's value types are transparent

`Out<K>`, `Sink` and `Seed` carry `#[repr(transparent)]`.

### 5. The controls are tests

`static_bump_memory.rs`: the threaded test's checks are one function, `first_broken`, over `(address, length, alignment)` blocks; `the_block_check_rejects_a_bump_that_aligns_by_offset` hands the same requests out over the same skewed buffer by offset alignment and asserts the function reports the misaligned block. `unit_panic_aborts.rs`: the boundary check is `aborted_at_the_boundary`, returning what it saw; the child entry `abort` aborts with no panic, and `the_boundary_check_rejects_an_abort_with_no_panic` asserts the check rejects it.

### 6. `from_raw_parts` has a test

`a_provider_over_raw_parts_is_dropped_before_its_block_is_freed` builds the provider over a boxed block with `from_raw_parts`, writes and reads a block of it, drops the provider, then frees the box: the order the benches keep.
