## N1: the unit-outer count model

The wide differential's unit-outer count expected every unit of an accumulator carrier to run once on every core whose slice of the N-way tiling is not empty. No unit of the fixture declares `COMMUTATIVE`, which defaults to false (`hilavitkutin-api/src/work_unit.rs:89`), so that expectation is the split of non-commutative fibers across cores. The ratified row `ordering_is_guaranteed_by_construction` (the spec `:604-607`) says "a non-commutative fiber runs on a single core", and `a_fiber_is_commutative_only_if_all_its_units_are_and_resource_accumulation_uses_per_thread_accumulators_merged_after` (ratified) gives multi-core morsel dispatch to commutative fibers only. The model was fitted to `accum_slice` as it is, and the review is right that it is wrong: the expected value comes from the canon.

Under the canon a unit runs on `run_parallel` as often as on `run`, at every worker count. That holds of the non-commutative carrier, which is not split, and at one worker it is `the_engine_is_one_and_the_core_count_is_configuration` (ratified): the same code paths at one core as at seven. The review proposes the one-worker divergence (one walk over the whole range where `run` walks each window) as a question to op. It is not one: the ratified row answers it, and the repair is job 5's one frame body (ticket 115, `hilavitkutin_ships_three_run_entries_where_the_unified_engin`). Asking op what a ratified row says is the shape the workspace rules refuse.

So the unit-outer count test holds `run_parallel` to `run`'s counts, red today at frame 0 for the split (ticket 116, `the_ungated_n_way_record_split_ships_where_its_own_round_del`, which job 9 restates as the commutativity gate) and for the two bodies (115), and at frame 1 for the skip (157). It is ignored under the 116 row, the gap that stays after job 5, with the other two named in its reason. The tiling arithmetic and its test go.

## N2: counts from the cone, and the record-less stores

Every build's counts are judged against the skip rule directly: a unit in the cone of a step runs as often as `run` ran it in the cold frame, and a unit outside it not at all, the unjudged units left out as the cone check leaves them. `run` and `run_parallel` go through one judge, so `run_parallel` is no longer held to an entry the suite shows wrong. The record-less count test turns red at frame 1 (`run_parallel` runs every unit on a clean frame), ignored under 157.

The record-less build has no record, so its stores test compares empty with empty and cannot fail on stores. It is deleted; the record-less build keeps the cone and count tests, the two it can fail.
