# loop-core-review-one

This round answers the first review of the loop core round (`202611130153-yegv`, closed at 8405887) on the same branch, `work/loop-core`. The review held the branch back on nine findings and a list of shapes not safe to lock. It found the seals, the ledger hashes, the mutants and the canon handling sound, and this round keeps them.

The repository declares no canon, so the designs govern, and the plan the designs answer is roadmap step HL0a of the engine roadmap r7 with its sketches S1 and S3. Where this round departs from that plan it says so in the doc changelist, under its own heading.

## What the review asks

The staged side of a step is derived from the checked table, one part per unit, `Send`, split by the unit's write set and by position range, with the committed view `Sync`, so that a threaded runner edits no author and no implementor. The checked table is branded to the store it was checked against. A kernel names one role per column, and the encoding is found without specialization, on `notko-hlist`'s `PlaceOf`, with a fall-back stated. The law matrix runs on two or more columns, with mutants for a store that ignores the column and a swap that drops unwritten columns. A law fails, with a witness, when handed fewer than two runs. The assertion that cannot fail goes. The frozen lints lose their escape. The ledger check becomes a lint and covers the manifest and any build script. The root README and the agent template's crate count are put right.

Five shapes are fixed now: `FormatId::new` is public and unchecked, `KernelId::new` panics on data, `Count::sub` wraps, a visit of morsel size zero is undefined, and `Index` stands for three different things. Five are recorded as not locked, each with a ticket: the push-callback visit, the domain's `first`, `next` and `reach`, positions as dense key-order ranks with no resize, `Word` as the storage unit with `Cell64` the widest cell, and a kernel's values as nested pairs.

## Not decided here

Which of the five unlocked shapes change, and to what. Fusion, threading beyond the proof that a threaded runner needs nothing new, and the close of roadmap step HL0b.
