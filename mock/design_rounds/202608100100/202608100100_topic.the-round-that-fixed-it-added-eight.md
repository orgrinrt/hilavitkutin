# The round that closed the collision added eight violations of its own

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** `hilavitkutin-sym`

Round nine took the scoped gate from five findings to thirteen. Every one of the
eight is mine, and all of them are the same two shapes.

**`PartialEq::eq` returns `bool`**, twice, in the hand-written impls that
replaced the derives. That is a std trait method signature, which the rules name
as a definition-site exception, so it is a legitimate use needing a stated reason
rather than a redesign.

**The two new laws use `u32` and `u8`** where they compare a raw id against a
width and enumerate the sixteen origins. Both cross arvo's own `from_raw` and
`to_raw` boundary, which is the other declared exception.

So all eight want the same treatment: a same-line `lint:allow` naming both lints
with a reason and a tracked id, pinned with `#[rustfmt::skip]` where the line
ends in a brace.

The reason this is its own round rather than a footnote is that leaving it would
be the exact pattern the last three rounds each criticised in the one before:
work the report, do not sweep the class, let a number drift upward while
declaring the substantive thing fixed. Round nine closed a real collision. That
does not buy it eight free violations.
