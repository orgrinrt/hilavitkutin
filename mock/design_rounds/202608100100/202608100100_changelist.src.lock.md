# Src changelist: eight allows, at their definition sites

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Phase:** IMPL

## CHANGE: `PartialEq::eq` carries its std signature with a reason

Files: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/src/handle.rs`,
`mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/src/kind.rs`

`fn eq(&self, other: &Self) -> bool` is a std trait method signature, which the
no-bare-primitive rule names as a definition-site exception. It is a use rather
than a choice: the trait declares the return type and an impl cannot change it.

Both sites get a same-line allow naming both lints, with `#[rustfmt::skip]`
because the line ends in a brace and the formatter would otherwise move the
comment into the body where the lint does not read it.

## CHANGE: the two new laws' raw arithmetic

File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/src/shape.rs`

`every_origin_fits_the_id_field` compares a raw id against a width, and enumerates
the sixteen origins through `Uint::from_raw`. Both cross arvo's own conversion
boundary, the other declared exception, and both carry reasons.

## Why this is a round rather than a footnote

Round nine took the scoped gate from five to thirteen. All eight are mine, and
leaving them would be exactly what the last three rounds each criticised in the
one before: work the report, do not sweep the class, let the number drift while
declaring the substantive thing fixed.

Verification: `mock --lint-only --strict --scope hilavitkutin-sym,hilavitkutin-str`
reports zero `no-bare-numeric` and zero `arvo-types-only`, measured after the
autofix rather than predicted here.
