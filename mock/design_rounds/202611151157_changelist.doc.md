## Where the pack lives, and why it has std

The ratified tenet `every_crate_is_no_std_with_no_allocation_and_the_consumer_provides_the_memory_provider` is about the crates a consumer builds on. A lint pack is a mockspace plug-in: it implements the engine's lint and tool traits, reads manifests and writes files, and cannot be `no_std`. It is therefore kept out of `<mock>/crates/` and out of the workspace's members, under `<mock>/tools/`, where `cross-target` already is a std tool with a manifest of its own. `hilavitkutin-build` itself stays `no_std` and touches no file: `Prototype::write_script` and `write_wrapper` write into a `core::fmt::Write`, as the provisional tenet `the_build_crate_is_no_std_takes_the_profile_and_target_strings_as_parameters_and_writes_its_text_into_a_formatting_writer` has it, and the pack is the one that puts the text on disk.

How hilavitkutin's own workspace takes the pack, by a `[lint-crates]` line naming its own repository or by the discovery of `<mock>/tools/`, is read off mockspace's tool contract in the source changelist. A consumer takes it by the `[lint-crates]` line either way.
