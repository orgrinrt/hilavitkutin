## Current state

`StaticBumpMemory` has the one constructor `new` over a `&'static mut [MaybeUninit<u8>]`. Four benches (`engine_vs_std`, `axis_ce`, `rcm_common`, `frame-wide-support`) each build it over a `'static` borrow they make up from a box their own `Drop` frees, and the provider is still alive when the box goes. The threaded provider test checks only that blocks are disjoint, never aligned or inside the buffer. The two panic tests in `tests/unit_panic_aborts.rs` start a child and assert nothing of how it ended. `dispatch/wu_fn.rs` states a cost for the unwind boundary that nothing measured.

`tests/run_parallel_skip_differential.rs` holds `run_parallel` to the skip on four units, at most two trunk roots in a phase and one window of records, and judges ran-or-not rather than counts; its doc says an odd worker count places the units differently, which that fixture does not show. Nothing holds `run_parallel` to the skip on the windowed walk over several windows, on the record-less walk, or on the unit-outer walk an accumulator writer puts a frame on.

The providers crate's own docs name `builder.add_resource(...)` and `add_kit`, which the builder does not have; its registration method is `with` (`hilavitkutin/src/scheduler/builder.rs:84`). `lib.rs` says the crate ships the interner surface alone.

## Changes proposed

### 1. `StaticBumpMemory::from_raw_parts`

`hilavitkutin-providers/src/memory.rs` gains `pub const unsafe fn from_raw_parts(base: *mut MaybeUninit<u8>, len: USize) -> Self`, with a `# Safety` section stating the caller's contract as the design states it, and `new` delegates to it over the borrowed slice. The module, type, `Send`/`Sync` and promise-2 comments argue the promises over both constructors.

### 2. The benches hold their buffer for the provider's life

In each of the four benches `HeapBump` keeps its provider in a `ManuallyDrop<StaticBumpMemory>`, builds it with `from_raw_parts` over the boxed block, and its `Drop` drops the provider before it frees the block. Three of them check clean; `axis_ce` fails on two copies of `notko` before and after this change (task `32aa15243d4e`). That the four carry the same `HeapBump` is task `d025c4344dae`.

### 3. The provider and panic tests hold what they claim

`tests/static_bump_memory.rs`: the threaded test is `concurrent_callers_get_aligned_disjoint_blocks_inside_the_buffer`, over a buffer three bytes past a 64-byte boundary, and it asserts every block aligned by its address, inside the buffer, and apart from every other. `tests/unit_panic_aborts.rs`: each test runs the frame in a re-executed child of its own binary, drains the child's stderr on a thread, kills it at 30 s, and asserts it ended on `SIGABRT` with the unit's panic message printed before the refusal to unwind. Both controls are in `mock/research/202611140613_review-jobs-one-to-three/controls.txt`.

### 4. `wu_fn.rs` states no cost it did not measure

The sentence on what the boundary costs under `panic = "abort"` and under unwinding is dropped.

### 5. The skip on every worker walk, `tests/run_parallel_wide_differential.rs`

`tests/skip_wide/` is a fixture of six producers, each reading its own `Resource<In<K>>` and a seed column and writing `Out<K>`, a consumer of `Out<0>`, and an accumulator writer appending the odd records of `Out<1>`, over 13000 records, more than one window of every fiber. It counts every unit's executions in atomics behind one lock, drives an eight-step history (one root, the consumer's root, two roots at once with the writer's among them, the same value again under a mark, every root, clean frames between), and holds the stores to a model independent of the engine.

The test file builds it three ways: `windowed` and `record_less` as plain builds over 13000 records and over none, and `unit_outer` with the accumulator writer. Each build has these, at 1, 2, 3, 4 and `MAX_CORES` workers through `FixedWorkers` where a pool is involved:

- `stores`: the every-store-dirty `run_parallel` equals the model, and the skipping one equals it, at every frame. Green on all three builds.
- `cone`: `run` runs exactly the cone of each step, as often as in the cold frame. Green on `windowed` and `unit_outer`, where the writer and the producer sharing its fiber are left unjudged (row `a_mixed_carrier_keeps_incremental_skip_per_fiber_and_accumulator_fibers_always_run_the_whole_range`, provisional, and question `does_a_skipped_accumulator_writer_run_anyway_in_hilavitkutin`). Red on `record_less` at frame 1, where `run` runs producer 0 on a clean frame, committed ignored under the new catalogue row `hilavitkutin_run_skips_nothing_on_a_record_less_frame` (task `de1e9491ca7d`).
- `count`: `run_parallel` runs each unit as often as `run`'s count says on its walk, the same count on the windowed and record-less walks and once on every core with a slice on the unit-outer walk. Green on `record_less`; red on `windowed` and `unit_outer` at frame 1, producer 0 running on a clean frame, ignored under `run_parallel_dispatches_every_unit_each_pass_and_never_asks`.
- `unmarked`: on the builds with records, an input changed without a mark leaves the skipping run behind the every-store-dirty one. Red on both at every count, ignored under the same row.

Beside them: `the_comparison_reports_a_runner_that_altered_one_record` is the store check's control, a run whose sink record 0 is altered after frame 3, reported as exactly that; `the_windowed_build_walks_several_windows_over_six_roots` asserts every producer runs more than once in the cold frame and the plan has six trunks; `the_unit_outer_tiling_counts_the_cores_with_a_slice` asserts the tiling arithmetic the unit-outer count rests on.

`tests/run_parallel_skip_differential.rs`'s doc says what its fixture places and walks, and points to this file. `mock/catalogue.md` gains the row.

### 6. The providers crate names `with`

`lib.rs`, `interner.rs`, `metrics/kit.rs` and `metrics/mod.rs` name `builder.with(...)`, `.with(MetricsKit::default())` and `.with(Resource::new(...))` in place of `add_resource` and `add_kit`, and `lib.rs` names everything the crate ships, with the clock and the operating-system memory provider under the engine's `platform-os` feature, as the readme now does. The same names in the api crate's source and in the other templates are triage item 27, the api lane's.
