# suite-can-fail-review-fixes

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** `mock/crates/hilavitkutin/tests/`, `hilavitkutin-api/src`, `hilavitkutin/src/resource/bindings/mod.rs`
**Source:** the first review pass of round 202611122301 (suite-can-fail), verdict do not merge

Round 202611122301 made the engine's suite able to fail. Its first review pass found the committed `ui` fixtures red, the `run_fused` refusal pair not establishing what its changelist claimed, and a few residuals. This round fixes what the review found and nothing else. The review's own words were returned in the session and are not a file; each finding is restated here with the evidence the fix answers.

## Findings and what answers each

1. The three `.stderr` files carry line numbers from before the commit hook's rustfmt expanded the fixtures, so `--test ui` failed three of six cases on the committed tree. Answer: format the fixtures first, then bless, then commit the formatted pair.
2. The `append_fused` pair shows that a unit without a per-record map is refused by `run_fused`, and the changelist claimed more: that appends cannot reach the fused entry. They can. A unit that implements `RecordOp` and also writes an accumulator compiles through `run_fused`, the fused `ChainWu` never runs its `execute`, and the append is silently dropped. Answer: the pair is renamed for what it shows (`unmapped_unit_fused`), the hole is a committed two-frame append test on `run_fused` that fails today and is ignored with its ticket (`0fb716c5a463`), and H25 is reported ticketed rather than closed.
3. Two comments in `resource/bindings/mod.rs` still say an append saturates at capacity, and the changelist's class grep was written narrowly enough to miss them and also matched an unrelated line. Answer: the two comments are fixed and the grep is rewritten around the property (an append described as a saturate or a drop) with the unrelated arithmetic line named as the one excluded hit.
4. The `StableProvider` in `tests/incremental_skip.rs`, and the providers in the `ui` fixtures, align the offset and not the address, over a `Vec<u8>` with alignment one. Answer: align the address.
5. The two stub placeholders carry their ticket ids in prose, not as `FIXME(<ticket id>): <reason>`, and the `HybridExecutor::run` comment contradicts itself ("observes the shutdown signal" then "observes nothing") and cites a pass that has since landed. Answer: the greppable marker form and a corrected comment.
6. `empty_build` and `scheduler_constructs_via_build` in `tests/scheduler_builder.rs` have identical bodies. Answer: delete one with the reason.
7. The close of the first round archived two open topics the changelist named as staying open. Answer: they were refiled by hand, and the tool fault is ticket `7dc2e880df64`. This round names both topics, and a second close will sweep them again until the pin is moved.

## Not in this round

The engine change that would make `run_fused` refuse an accumulator-writing carrier (`0fb716c5a463`), the test providers that move with the scheduler (`6393002dea2d`), and the `add_resource` advice in the committed diagnostics (`71f89b0d7775`).
