## Current state

Step 6 `steps::spectral_partition` (`plan/steps.rs`) is a stub returning `FiberGrouping::new()`; its output is discarded by the runner (`mod.rs:392`). arvo-spectral's `SparseLaplacian` walks forward edges only (the directed Laplacian) and assumes `W: Into<F>` numeric weights, neither of which fits the engine's directed-dependency, `EdgeKind`-valued graph for spectral partitioning.

## Change

Add an engine-local `SymmetricLaplacian` (`plan/laplacian.rs`) that wraps the `CsrBidirectional` and implements `arvo_spectral::LinearOperator` with the symmetric unit-weight matvec: `(L x)[i]` is the sum over undirected neighbours of `(x[i] - x[j])`, where undirected neighbours are successors plus predecessors. No deduplication is needed because the graph is a DAG by step 6 (topo sort and the cycle check pass first), so successors and predecessors are disjoint per node. This is composition over the `LinearOperator` contract, not reinvention of a substrate primitive. `spectral_partition` builds the operator from `graph.to_csr_bidirectional()`, computes the Gershgorin `lambda_max` bound (`max_i 2 * deg(i)`), runs `arvo_spectral::k_way_partition` over it (`K = MAX_FIBERS`, iterations 100), and maps the partition ids onto a `FiberGrouping`.
