## Intent

Document that single-core `Scheduler::run` now drives the const-gated per-trunk
dispatch built on the shipped `RunGatedTrunk` walk, replacing the flat `RunFiber`
drive. The DESIGN currently frames the per-trunk walk and the nested
`RunTrunk`/`RunPhase`/`RunPipeline` carrier as "a following round" and states
`Scheduler::run` "continues to drive the flat single-core walk"; G-e lands the
per-trunk dispatch as the actual single-core path. The nested-carrier walk stays
a parallel exploration (it consumes the walled nested type); the const-gated
per-trunk dispatch over the flat carrier is the canonical mechanism (op mandate
#672, roadmap r4 stage G-e).
