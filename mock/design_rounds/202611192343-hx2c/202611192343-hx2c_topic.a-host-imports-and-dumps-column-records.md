# Topic: a host imports records into a column and dumps them out between frames

**Date:** 2026-11-19
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** `hilavitkutin` (`Scheduler`, the design section on the host data path), its tests
**Source topics:** loru-coord's ask 1, ticket `7c222795cf39`; the expert seat's read, `mock/research/202611191955_consumer-surface-seat/NOTES.md` section 1; stack-coord's job `hila-consumer-surface`

## What the record says

The canon names the host path: column data moves out of the engine and into it through evict and dump, and inject and import (`tenet::column_data_moves_out_of_and_into_the_engine_through_evict_dump_and_inject_import`, ratified). The consumer provides the backing memory of a column and storage is reached by raw pointers (`tenet::the_consumer_provides_the_backing_memory_of_a_column`, ratified), and a single-column typed accessor is eliminated because the consumer reads storage directly (`tenet::a_single_column_typed_accessor_is_eliminated_because_the_consumer_reads_storage_directly`, ratified). Change detection is per morsel and the engine owns the counters, but the per-morsel counters are not built and the engine has a coarse per-store dirty bit only (`tenet::the_per_morsel_generation_counters_are_not_built_and_the_engine_has_a_coarse_per_store_dirty_only`, provisional, stamped). `Scheduler::mark_dirty` marks one store changed for the next frame.

## What the code does

`SchedulerBuilder::build` takes the consumer's `ColumnStorage` by value and keeps it; nothing gives the host a way to put records into a built scheduler's column or to read them out, other than the hidden `__bindings()` accessor. loru writes and reads columns between frames through that accessor.

## Where the record is silent

What a host import marks as changed, a record range or the whole store, is op's question `what_a_host_import_into_a_built_scheduler_marks_as_changed`. Who says that a store changed is open (`question::who_says_that_a_store_changed`).

## Decision for this round

Derived, not asked, from the R2 row. `Scheduler` gains `import` and `dump`, the copy half of R2, between frames only. A store is named by its marker type, `Column<T>` or `Buffered<T, M>`, and found through the same column selector a context projection walks. `import` copies a slice of records in at a position and marks the store changed with the coarse bit `mark_dirty` sets, since the per-morsel counters are not built; a range mark waits on them, and a method that takes a range and marks the whole store is not offered. `dump` copies a range out into a notko `BulkPush` sink. A range past the store's record count is refused before anything is copied. Accumulators, virtuals and resources take neither: they have no place in the selector. The zero-copy half (the storage handed back, a column's store id) is left for a later round, ticketed.
