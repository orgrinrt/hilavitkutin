# Topic: the widths describe the layout, they do not constrain it

**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** `hilavitkutin-sym`. Makes the shape's declared widths load-bearing,
and removes the third recurrence of a test class three reviews have flagged.

## The finding, proved rather than argued

A reviewer constructed it: **a shape declaring `KIND_BITS = 9` and
`ID_BITS = 22` over `SymLayout`, whose bitfield is three and twenty-eight,
compiles and passes both shipped width laws.**

So the laws are over the declarations rather than over the thing declared. The
previous round removed `ORIGINS` because nothing read it and called that the fix.
It was the symptom. The four surviving widths have the same defect: a type
declares something about itself, no code reads it, no check ties it to what it
describes, and any value compiles.

That is the tautological-test failure moved out of the suite and into the type
surface. It reads as a contract and is a comment with a type.

## And the test class is back a third time

`assert_eq!(Standard::KIND_BITS.0, 3)` restates the literal declared in the same
file. `assert!(0b10000 > 0b111)` compares two literals. Both are structurally
incapable of failing.

Two prior reviews flagged this class, the previous round's topic said it deletes
such tests rather than repairing them, and its changelist carried the verification
*"no assertion in the crate compares a shape constant to the literal its own impl
declares"*. **That verification is false against `HEAD`.** The round that
diagnosed an unrun check, wrote the post-autofix discipline into its own header,
and then shipped a false one on first use.

The common cause, in the reviewer's words: assertions are being written to
describe the code rather than to constrain it. Naming that is the point of this
round, because three rounds have now fixed instances of it and none fixed the
habit.

## What this round does

**Tie the widths to the layout.** `SymLayoutOps` gains the widths its carrier
actually has, and a law asserts each shape's declared width equals its layout's.
A shape that lies then fails to satisfy its own law, which is what makes the
declaration a constraint.

**Delete the two restatements**, and check by construction that none remains
rather than by asserting it: the check is a grep against `git show HEAD` for the
shape of the failure, not a sentence saying it was done.

**Clear the mechanical remainder**: the design document still omits
`Domain::Shape`, the mechanism this branch's central fix rests on, and gives two
other signatures that drifted. The README claims a build-dependency stub this
branch itself removed. Seven `lint:allow` comments were moved off their item
lines by rustfmt. A commit subject is 74 characters, and `partition` is a banned
term used four times in newly written prose.

## What this round does not do

The generated `docs/` tree still disagrees with its templates. Regenerating
renames every overview document under a newer mockspace convention, which is a
real migration and does not belong inside a sym fix. It is recorded as owed
rather than done, and the reviewer is right that it is drift either way.

## Lock criteria

- A shape whose declared widths contradict its layout **fails to compile or
  fails a law**, verified by writing one and observing the failure, then deleting
  it.
- Zero assertions comparing a constant to the literal its own definition sets,
  verified by grep against `git show HEAD`.
- Every claim in this round's changelist verified against `git show HEAD` after
  the autofix, output recorded, including the claim that no such assertion
  remains.
- `cargo build` warning-free from this crate; banned vocabulary absent from
  prose this branch wrote.
