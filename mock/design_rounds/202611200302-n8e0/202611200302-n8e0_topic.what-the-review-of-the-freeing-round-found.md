# What the review of the freeing round found

**Date:** 2026-11-20
**Phase:** topic
**Scope:** `hilavitkutin-api` and `hilavitkutin-providers` column storage reserve, `MemoryArena`'s entry bound, `StaticFreeListMemory`'s text and tests, the str refusal boundary's tests, the platform sections' layout

The review of round `202611200201-yszg` found the free-list provider correct as written and left a set of gaps around it, which this round closes before the branch lands.

`ArenaColumnStorage::reserve` gives a live id's block back before it checks the arguments, so a re-reserve that overflows its byte length destroys the column and answers `LengthOverflow` without asking the provider for anything. The design says a re-reserve gives the old block back before it takes the new one, and says nothing of what a refused re-reserve leaves. The round settles it: an argument the store refuses (an id past the cap, a length past `usize`) leaves the column as it was, and a block the provider refuses leaves the id unreserved with a count of zero, since the old block went back first so the new one could take its place.

`MemoryArena` refuses an `ENTRIES` of 2^28 at build, which the yszg design justified as keeping every issued id below the refusal. An arena of 2^28 entries issues ids up to 2^28 - 1, which is below the refusal and fits the 28-bit field, and the arena's own refusal is `u32::MAX`, not an id in the field. The bound was one short, as ticket `the_memory_arena_s_entry_bound_is_one_short_of_the_28_bit_id_field` says; the round takes it to `ENTRIES <= 2^28` and closes the ticket.

Four laws of the yszg round stay green under a mutation that breaks them, so their tests are widened: the order of give-back and take on a re-reserve, the space in front of a block aligned above the grain going back on the list, the list's lay-down over a buffer that does not start or end on the grain, and the id just below the str refusal being accepted. The free-list provider's two atomics get their orderings named in its text, since the ordering table the provisional row `every_cross_thread_atomic_of_the_design_stands_in_one_table_with_its_ordering_on_each_side` asks for does not exist in the designs yet.

The platform sections' reflowed lines are brought back to the wrap width, and the `lint:allow` of the provided `allocate_zeroed` moves to its signature as every sibling keeps it.
