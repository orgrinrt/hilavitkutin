# Doc changelist: accumulator columns

**Date:** 2026-06-04
**Phase:** DOC
**Round:** 202606041800 (accumulator-columns, branch `feat/hilavitkutin-accumulator-columns`)

The round adds the accumulator store kind (append-during-frame columns) as the second half of op's input-vs-accumulator model. The engine DESIGN prose enumerates the store kinds and describes the per-WorkUnit Context accessors and the `build` drain; these gain the accumulator kind, the append accessor, and the accumulator drain arm. The new types (`Accum<T>`, `AccumBinding`, `AccumSelector`, `AccumProject`, `AccumWriterApi`) land in the SRC phase; the design-doc-source-mismatch check validates them against source at round close.

## File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

### Current state

- The per-WorkUnit Context paragraph says the `EngineCtx` "implements the seven `HasX` accessor traits", "resolves resources from the arena, reads and writes columns over the per-frame column buffers, and iterates the morsel".
- The `build` paragraph describes the `Column<T>` drain arm reserving a real column sized by the record count, with no accumulator arm.

### Changes proposed

- Update the Context accessor paragraph: an `EngineCtx` now also appends to accumulator columns. Bump "seven" to "eight" `HasX` accessor traits (the new `HasAccumWriter`), and add the append to the accessor list.
- Update the `build` paragraph: alongside the `Column<T>` drain arm, the `Accum<T>` drain arm reserves a real column sized by the record count (capacity this round) and records the `ColumnPtr<T>` plus a `Cell<USize>` live-length in the `AccumBinding`; the live-length grows from zero as WorkUnits append, and a later PassEnd drain reads `[0, live_length)`.

## Out of scope (doc stays as-is)

- The PassEnd persistence-drain prose (#344 / #134).
- A distinct per-accumulator capacity dimension.
