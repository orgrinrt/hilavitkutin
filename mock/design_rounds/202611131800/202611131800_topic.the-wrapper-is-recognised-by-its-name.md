# The wrapper is recognised by its name

Second pass of the local review of this branch, after round 202611131745.

## Current state

`configure_with` treats any non-empty `RUSTC_WORKSPACE_WRAPPER` as the wrapper. `cargo clippy` sets it to `clippy-driver`, and a cache such as sccache can be the workspace wrapper too, so in those builds the flags are dropped and nothing warns. The `lint:allow(no-bare-string)` markers the src changelist of 202611131745 said were moved onto signature lines were put back on comment lines by rustfmt, where the lint does not read them, and `flags.rs` added two more of the kind. The close of 202611131745 swept the two flat topics of other work into its directory again; they are moved back flat here.

## Changes proposed

1. The wrapper counts as present only when the path cargo names ends with `hilavitkutin-rustc-wrapper`. The design says so, and a test feeds `clippy-driver`.
2. Every `no-bare-string` marker on a comment line is deleted. The lints are run; a marker comes back only where the lint then fires, on the line it reads.
