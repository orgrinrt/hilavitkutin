## The `lint:allow` markers on comment lines

Every marker that sat alone on a comment line in `bootstrap.rs`, `flags.rs`, `pragma.rs`, `profile.rs` and `requirements.rs` is deleted: fourteen of them, put there or moved there by rustfmt, which takes a trailing comment after `{` onto the next line, where `line_lint_allowed` does not read it. The lints were run with them gone. No error fired; the warn-level findings at the lines they were meant for (`no-bare-string`, `no-bare-option`, `no-public-raw-field`, `no-bare-numeric`) were firing already and stay visible. `no-bare-static-str` does not fire on `LOOP_OPTIMIZATION`'s `[&str; 5]`, so its marker goes too; `RUSTFLAGS_VAR` keeps its marker on its own line, where the lint reads it.
