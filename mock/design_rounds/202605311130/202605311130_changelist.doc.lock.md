# Doc changelist

The scheduler-build prose in `DESIGN.md.tmpl` (updated last round for
topological dispatch) says `build` computes the plan and stores its
topological permutation. It does not say the plan itself persists. This round
store-backs the plan's flat pools, so the prose gains that: the plan's flat

### CHANGE: scheduler-build paragraph (the plan-compute + drain passage)

The passage that reads "Before draining the store arena, `build` computes the
execution plan from the registered bundle and stores its topological
permutation on the scheduler" gains a sentence: after extracting the
permutation and draining the resource columns, `build` reserves the plan's
flat pools (phases, trunks, fibers, per-unit metadata, per-fiber morsel sizes,
the RCM renumber) as columns in the same `ColumnStorage`, at a `StoreId`
namespace continued past the resource columns, and keeps a `Copy` `PlanHandle`
(base id plus live counts) so the plan persists for the dispatch consumer
instead of being dropped after `build`. Per-fiber column classification and
the dirty masks stay off the store this round (their columnar form and their
consumers are later rounds).

New backticked name introduced here that must exist as a pub item in source:
`PlanHandle` (pub struct in `scheduler/mod.rs`). `ColumnStorage`, `StoreId`,
`BuildError` already exist.
