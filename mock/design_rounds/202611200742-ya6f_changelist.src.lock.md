## Changes

### 1. The free list's accessor returns a raw pointer

`crates/hilavitkutin-providers/src/memory/free_list.rs`: the private `unsafe fn list(&self)` returns `*mut List` in place of `&mut List`, and its `#[allow(clippy::mut_from_ref)]` goes. It still lays the first node down on first use under the caller's lock, and hands back the list's address. `allocate` and `release` each take the head's address with `&raw mut (*list).head` in an unsafe block of its own, with a SAFETY comment saying the pointer is the provider's own list and the lock is held. Nothing else in the file moves.
