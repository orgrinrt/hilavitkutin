# src changelist: the swap fixtures read what their units write

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Phase:** SRC
**Topic:** `202611131407_topic.every-swap-fixture-field-is-read.md`
**Doc changelist:** `202611131408_changelist.doc.lock.md`

Seven `#[allow(dead_code)]` attributes go, the ones `work/recover-landing-swap` adds over `dev`. No engine source changes: the three files are a bench variant crate and two test files.

## CHANGE: `mock/benches/variants/swap_common/src/lib.rs`

The attribute on `C1`, `C2`, `C3` and `Cp` is removed. Each is a `pub struct` with a `pub` field in a library, so nothing about it is dead and the attribute did nothing. The crate builds with no warning of its own.

## CHANGE: `tests/replace_swap.rs` reads `Out`

The attribute on `Out` goes. `FillWu` wrote the record index into its column and nothing looked at it. `replace_resource_opens_the_plan_band_replace_value_does_not` now poisons the column with `Out(u32::MAX)` before the cold frame and, after it, holds every record to its index. The band decisions the test reads are then read on frames of a carrier shown to dispatch.

Shown failing first: with `FillWu` skipping record 0 the test fails on `out rec 0`. The unit was put back.

## CHANGE: `tests/replace_swap.rs` reads `ZOut`

The attribute on `ZOut` goes. `ZstReaderWu` wrote the record index into its column and the test counted its executions only. `zst_swap_seeds_its_reader_for_the_next_frame` now poisons the column with `ZOut(u32::MAX)` after the clean frame, before the swap, and after the frame that follows the swap holds every record to its index. The count says the reader was entered, and the column says it covered its records.

Shown failing first: with `ZstReaderWu` skipping record 0 the test fails on `zout rec 0`. The unit was put back.

## CHANGE: `tests/replace_swap_plan_band.rs` reads `Av` and `Pv`

The attribute on `Av` goes. `ConsumerWu` wrote 7 to every record and nothing looked. Each arm now poisons the column before its cold frame and holds every record to 7 after it, through the macro `assert_consumer_covered!`, on `run` and on `run_parallel`.

`Pv` carried no attribute, and the compiler reports its field as never read: the plan unit declares a write to its column and its body writes nothing, so the type was never constructed. Whether that warning was already printed at the refused tip was not measured. A dead field left with a warning is the same thing as one hidden by an attribute, so it is closed too. Both columns are poisoned by `poison_columns!`, and at the end of each arm `assert_plan_column_untouched!` holds every record of the plan unit's column to the poison. That checks what the fixture's own comment states, that the unit writes nothing.

Shown failing first, both: with `ConsumerWu` skipping record 0 the test fails on `run: consumer rec 0`, and with `PlanWu` writing its column the test fails on `run: plan rec 0 changed`. Both units were put back.

Pointers are taken with their record types (`*mut Av`, `*mut Pv`, `*const Out`, `*const ZOut`), so a wrong depth in the bindings walk does not compile.
