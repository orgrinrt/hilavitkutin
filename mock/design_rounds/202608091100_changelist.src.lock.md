# Src changelist: FieldWidth, the span law, the accessor suite, a dead gate

**Date:** 2026-08-09
**Phase:** IMPL
**Doc CL:** `202608091100_changelist.doc.lock.md`

Built tests first. Each law below is written and watched fail, against the
construction that motivated it, with the offending shape named in the failure,
before the code that satisfies it exists. A law that has never failed has not
been shown to constrain anything, and that is the entire history of this defect.

## CHANGE: add `FieldWidth`, replacing the mask constants as the width source

File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/src/shape.rs`

`width_of(mask)` and the two forwarded mask constants go. In their place a
`FieldWidth` trait with `const WIDTH: USize`, blanket-implemented over `Bits`,
so the width of a field is read off the type its accessor is compiled against.

Why this rung and not the previous one: `KIND_MASK` was forwarded by hand and
read by nothing outside the law that checked it, so a wrong mask was silent.
`Self::Kind` is the return type of `get_kind`, whose body returns what the
bitfield hands it, so a wrong associated type is a compile error.

Evidence, both by construction and both recorded because the first is what the
previous round believed:

- `WideKindLayout` left genuinely five bits of kind over twenty-six of id, with
  forwarded masks of popcount nine and twenty-two and a shape declaring nine and
  twenty-two: **all sixteen tests passed**.
- The same layout with `type Kind = Bits<9, Hot>` over the five-bit field:
  **four `E0308`s**.

Verification, run against `git show HEAD` after the autofix rather than against
the working tree: `grep -c 'KIND_MASK\|ID_MASK\|width_of'` returns 0, and
`grep -c 'FieldWidth for Bits'` returns 1.

**The grep is deliberately not pinned to the keyword order.** A first attempt
searched for `impl<...> const FieldWidth`, which returned 0 against a commit
that carries the impl, because rustfmt rewrote it to `const impl<...>
FieldWidth`. The fact was true and the stated check was false, which is the
third time this exact trap has fired here. `FieldWidth for Bits` is the part the
formatter does not own.

Observed by construction after the change: the counter-example above, which
passed under round five, now fails with `WideKind declares a kind width its
layout does not carry`. And a lying `type Kind` is still four `E0308`s, so both
routes are closed rather than one traded for the other.

## CHANGE: tie `COUNTER_BITS` to the mechanism that uses it

File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/src/shape.rs`, tests

`ORIGIN_BITS` and `COUNTER_BITS` were tied only to each other and to `ID_BITS`.
Two laws pinned their sum; nothing pinned the split. `SixteenMinters` declaring
a five-bit origin over a twenty-three-bit counter passed all sixteen tests while
its own `origin_base` shifted by twenty-four.

New law, over every shape and every origin:

```
origin_ceiling(o) - origin_base(o) == 2^COUNTER_BITS - 1
```

That reads the span off the two functions that define it, so a wrong
`COUNTER_BITS` fails whatever the other declarations say. `ORIGIN_BITS` then
follows from the existing sum law, which becomes load-bearing once its other
term is pinned to a fact.

Verification: written against the lying split first and observed failing with
`SixteenMinters at origin MinterId(UFixed(Bits(0))) owns a span its COUNTER_BITS
does not describe`, `left: 16777215, right: 8388607`. The split was then restored
and the law is green.

## CHANGE: the accessor suite the crate never had

File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/tests/layout.rs` (new)

No test exercised any of the eight `SymLayoutOps` operations. Eight documented
methods, zero coverage.

Added, each over **both layouts** rather than the default alone.
`SixteenMinters` shares `SymLayout` with `Standard`, so two layouts is the whole
matrix at this level rather than a sample of three shapes; the shape-level laws
live beside the shapes and do run over all three.

- `every_accessor_round_trips_at_every_boundary`: kind, id and flag, at zero, at
  the field maximum, and at an alternating-bit value. The maximum finds a field
  one bit too narrow; the alternating value finds a shift in the wrong
  direction.
- `writing_one_field_disturbs_no_other`: the overlap case every prior law was
  blind to, because a law reading one field at a time cannot see two fields
  sharing a bit.
- `a_zeroed_layout_reads_zero_everywhere`.
- `layout_equality_is_raw_bit_equality`: the crate's stated central promise,
  asserted nowhere until now, checked in both directions and per field.
- `the_three_fields_together_are_the_whole_handle`: the other half of the
  overlap check, since disjointness alone passes on a layout leaving a bit
  unreachable.

**The overlap test was shown to discriminate**, which is the only evidence it
constrains anything. `WideKindLayout`'s kind was moved from bit 26 to 25, so it
collides with the id's top bit. Two tests failed, naming the field: "setting
kind to its maximum spilled into id".

**And every width law still passed under that overlap.** That is the claim the
design document makes, demonstrated rather than asserted: a law reading one
field at a time cannot see two fields share a bit.

## CHANGE: exhaustion and origin-span boundaries, from both sides

File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/tests/origins.rs`

- `a_minter_keeps_refusing_once_exhausted`: the existing test stopped at the

## CHANGE: remove a forbidden feature gate that enables nothing

File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-str/tests/handle.rs:3-4`

`#![feature(generic_const_exprs)]` is forbidden outright rather than watched.
The file uses nothing it provides: no `where [(); ...]` bound, no `cap_size`.
Both lines go; all seven tests pass without them, verified.

A sweep of both crates found no other forbidden gate. Every remaining one
(`const_trait_impl`, `macro_metavar_expr_concat`) is allowed or watched.

## CHANGE: fix the allows the fifth review found suppressing nothing

Files: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/src/kind.rs`,
`mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/src/shape.rs`,
`mock/crates/hilavitkutin-str/src/{hash.rs,../tests/ergonomics.rs}`

- `kind.rs`: the allow sits one line below its violation, inside the body, and
  carries no `#[rustfmt::skip]`, so it suppresses nothing. Both lints fire live.
- `shape.rs` at three sites: the allow names `no-bare-numeric` only, while
  `arvo-types-only` also fires and is unsuppressed.
- `hilavitkutin-str` at three sites: formatter-unstable, since `rustfmt::skip`
  was applied to `sym` alone.

Formatter stability was measured rather than reasoned about. `cargo fmt --check`
named ten unstable sites across the two crates; three carried allows on lines
ending in `{` and are now pinned with `#[rustfmt::skip]`. The rest was
pre-existing formatting drift unrelated to allows and was normalised. `cargo fmt
--check` now reports zero diffs.

Verification, measured rather than claimed: `mock --lint-only --strict --scope
hilavitkutin-sym,hilavitkutin-str` reports **five** findings, down from nine,
and **zero** `no-bare-numeric` or `arvo-types-only`.

The five that remain: four `semantic-alias-nudge` advisories asking for a domain
alias over `Bool` and `Bit` in four signatures, and one `writing-style`
advisory on generated agent documentation. None is a `no-bare-*` violation and
none is in this round's scope; they are named here so the count is not reported
as zero by a round that did not reach zero.
