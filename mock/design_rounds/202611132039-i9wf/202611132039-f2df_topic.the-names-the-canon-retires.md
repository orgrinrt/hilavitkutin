## Changes proposed

1. A name goes in `rules_out` when its every use in the tree is wrong: an identifier or a phrase, never a bare common word, which the matcher would find in any sentence. Four ratified tenets retire names of that kind, and each gets them:
   - `the_dead_terms_chain_chain_group_partition_archetype_entity_row_and_order_have_named_replacements`: `chain group`, which `chain_group` and `ChainGroup` also spell, since the matcher reads an identifier as the words it is made of.
   - `the_data_structures_of_the_analysis_are_named_phase_map_fiber_plan_and_trunk_plan`: `PartitionMap`, `ChainPlan` and `ChainGroupPlan`, the three names its passage says are replaced.
   - `scheduling_hints_replace_an_order_field_on_the_unit`: `order field`.
   - `there_is_no_std_platform_tier`: `std tier`.
2. The names the engine bans in its own crates stay out of `rules_out`: `dyn`, `std::any`, `TypeId`, `thread::spawn`. Scoped lints hold them where the ban reaches (`no-dyn-dispatch`, `forbidden-imports`, `no-runtime-spawn`, `engine-spawn-through-gate`). The tree uses them, rightly, where it does not: the lint interface of the pack takes a boxed trait object, a bench support crate keys its per-thread state by `std::any`, and the std baseline of `engine_vs_std` spawns its threads. The matcher also reads `TypeId` as the words of `type Id`, an associated type the symbol crate declares. The rest of the dead terms (`chain`, `partition`, `archetype`, `entity`, `row`, `order`) are bare common words and stay with `vocabulary-discipline`.
3. Ten lines state those names to say they are dead: the dead-term lists in `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, `mock/PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl`, the agent templates and the `vocabulary-discipline` lint, and the sentence of `mock/PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl` saying there is no std tier. Each cites the tenet that retires the name, in a comment within three lines of it, which is the reach in which the lint reads a line as being about the retirement. The words of the lines do not change.
4. The figure of round `202611131444` is corrected here: 207 entries on 137 rows were moved to `rejects`, not 238.
