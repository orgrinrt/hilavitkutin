## Current state

`mockspace.toml` declares the pack with no feature, so `cargo mock canon`, `already-said` and `unearth` do not exist here. The registry holds 841 tenets, 62 questions and 17 subjects.

Turning the feature on as the tree stands gives 4563 lint errors from four of the pack's seven registry lints.

`a-retired-word-stays-out` reads `rules_out` as words no design, code or content line may name. The readers who filled the rows wrote prose there (a design not taken, a reading refused), 238 entries on 137 rows, and the lint reads every word run of them against the tree. 4172 findings.

`a-tenet-cites-what-its-words-name` holds `cites` to the tenets a row's `what` and `because` name by id. The rows carry 450 relations there that the words do not name. 371 findings.

`a-tenet-names-a-subject` reads the row files by line and takes a quote line that opens with a bracket for a table header, so two rows whose `subject` follows such a quote read as having none. 2 findings.

`every-fixme-names-a-ticket` holds each placeholder marker to the id of a ticket in this registry. 13 markers cite none, and 5 cite a ticket of the workspace's tracker, which this registry does not have. 18 findings.

## Changes proposed

1. `mockspace.toml` turns the `workflow` feature on, names the pack by the repository's present name, declares the pack's kinds beside `subject`, `tenet` and `question`, and gives the tools that search what was said the directory of the maintainer's captured words.
2. A tenet gains the field `rejects`, and every entry now in `rules_out` moves there unchanged. `rules_out` takes the pack's meaning and is empty on every row: a word goes there in the change that clears the tree of it, and none has been cleared against these rows yet. The dead terms of the principles stay held by the repository's own `vocabulary-discipline` lint.
3. `cites` keeps only what a row's words name, and every other entry moves to `related` as a `tenet::` link, which is where the pack keeps a relation the words do not make. No relation is dropped.
4. `subject` is written beside `id` in every tenet row, so no quote can hide it from a line reader.
5. `cargo mock owed` files one task per group of placeholder markers and rewrites each marker to cite it. A task filed for markers that cited a ticket of the workspace's tracker names that ticket.
6. No lint severity changes and no exemption is added.
