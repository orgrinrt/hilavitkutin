## Problem

C1d-3a landed the per-block fiber-to-trunk-component projection with the greedy former for every block. The C1d-2 width-gate decision is that wide or complex pipelines should use spectral partitioning instead of greedy (the design history's "spectral beats greedy" analysis: equivalent for narrow chains, lower cut for complex fan-in/out, valuable at scale). `project_fiber_components` left a marked selection point for that wide arm. This round wires it.

## Resolution

The arvo dependency moves past the live-node-count fix (`4cec32a`, task #643), so `arvo_spectral`'s iterative methods iterate the live node count rather than the const cap. That makes the engine's `spectral_partition` (step 6) correct on the engine's loose graphs (`unit_count` below `MAX_UNITS`). The bump requires one adaptation: `arvo_spectral::LinearOperator` now has a required `live_dim` method, so the engine's `SymmetricLaplacian` gains a one-line override delegating to its `CsrBidirectional::node_count()`. The blast radius is exactly that one method (verified by building against the bumped dependency).

The width-gate then routes per-block in `project_fiber_components`: a block whose unit count exceeds the threshold (DESIGN.md.tmpl's "spectral when >5 fibers", applied to block unit count for now, tunable) uses the spectral former; narrow blocks keep the greedy `group_fibers_in_block`. The spectral former reuses the existing global `spectral_partition` (computed once over the whole graph; it respects block boundaries because disconnected blocks have independent Fiedler vectors) and filters its assignment to the block's units, remapping the block's distinct spectral ids to contiguous block-local FiberIds. A new `spectral_grouping_in_block` helper does the filter-and-remap, returning the same block-local `FiberGrouping` shape the greedy former returns, so the rest of the projection is unchanged.

## Scope

Greedy stays the narrow-block former and the spectral output shape matches it, so this round adds the wide arm without disturbing C1d-3a's narrow path. `Branch`/`Bridge` components and the non-id `Fiber` fields remain deferred. After this round, C1d is complete and the arc moves to C2 dispatch codegen.
