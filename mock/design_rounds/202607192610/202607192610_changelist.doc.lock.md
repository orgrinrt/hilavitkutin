## CHANGE: `hilavitkutin/DESIGN.md.tmpl` accumulator-region paragraph

The passage describes core c appending at offset `lo_c`, the regions tiling `[0, total)`, and the
post-phase forward compaction. It gains the invariant: **the worker's region boundaries and the
merge's read offsets are the same function of `(total, ncores, core)`, computed once and shared,
not the same arithmetic written in two places.**

That is worth stating in the design rather than only in code, because it is a property of the
mechanism rather than of one implementation. Any future rework of either side has to preserve it,
and a reader checking the merge against the worker should find the requirement named instead of
inferring it from two expressions that happen to match.
