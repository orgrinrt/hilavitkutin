# Src changelist: what kaski's engine needs enters the api in place

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Phase:** SRC
**Round:** 202611130617-h6mb (branch `work/api-revision`)
**Source topics:** `202611130617-h6mb_topic.revise-the-api-in-place.md`, `202611130617-h6mb_topic.value-form-domains-and-the-buffered-kind.md`

The source follows the locked doc changelist (`202611130617-h6mb_changelist.doc.lock.md`). The api crate comes first, since every other crate builds against it, then the derive, then the engine, the kit and the providers. Where the source had to say something the design does not, the departure is written under the change it belongs to with its reason and its ticket.

## CHANGE: the shared list crate carries the access sets
Files: `mock/Cargo.toml`, `mock/Cargo.lock`, `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/Cargo.toml`, `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src/access.rs`.
`notko-hlist` enters the workspace dependencies and the lock moves `notko` to the commit that carries it. `Empty`, `Cons`, `Contains`, `ContainsAll` and `Concat` are that crate's items, re-exported from `hilavitkutin_api::access` and the root. `AccessSet` is implemented for `Empty` and for `Cons<H: Member, T: AccessSet>` and carries `LEN`, the count of members. The api's own copies of the list types and of the two membership markers are deleted.

`Holds<S>` holds exactly where `Contains<S>` does, through one blanket impl marked `do_not_recommend`, and reports a missing store in the api's words. `HoldsAll<L>` is a walk of two impls, on `Empty` and on `Cons`, sealed by a private supertrait, and asks `Holds` of `Member::Store` at each step. Neither of its impls is marked `do_not_recommend`: with the mark the compiler stopped at `HoldsAll` and named no store, and without it the failure names the `Holds` of the store that is missing (`tests/trybuild/holds_all_missing.stderr`).

## CHANGE: members, the three wrappers and the window
Files: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src/member.rs` (new), `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src/lib.rs`, `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src/prelude.rs`.
`Member` with `type Store`. Every store marker stands for itself. `Reach<C, const N: Cap>`, `Written<C>` and `Differs<C>` are zero-sized markers with a `const fn new`, each a `Member` over a `Column` and over a `Buffered`, standing for the store it wraps. `Differs` asks `PartialEq` of the value type in its `Member` impl, so a list naming `Differs` over an incomparable type is no access set.

`Window<'a, T, N>` has `here`, `before::<K>` and `after::<K>`, all `const fn`. A `K` past `N` is refused by a const assertion, a monomorphisation error that `cargo check` does not report, so its two failing twins and the passing control are doctests on `Window`, which are built.

Departures. `ExtensionSurface<T>` and `DefaultRunCfg<O>` are `Member`s standing for themselves: both are registered into the store list, and the `build` bound asks `Stores: AccessSet`, which asks `Member` of every cell. `Window` gains `unsafe const fn over(base, at, count)`, since a runner outside the api crate has to build one per record, and the three wrappers gain `const fn new`, since a member is passed to `get` as a value (ticket 76a880867ba4 for both).

## CHANGE: the buffered column
Files: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src/store.rs`.
`Buffered<T, M = Snapshot>`, the two modes `Snapshot` and `Exchange`, and the sealed `BufferMode`. A buffered column registers as a store through `StoreDispatch` like the other markers and has a `const fn new`.

## CHANGE: the value function and its two products
Files: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src/values.rs` (new), `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src/record_op.rs`, `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src/macros.rs`, `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src/context.rs`, `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src/footprint.rs`.
`Readable`, `Writable`, `ReadSet`, `WriteSet`, `ReadVals`, `WriteVals`, `ResourceRead` and `rec!`, with the impls the design's table of members states. A bare `Column<T>` or `Buffered<T, M>` is `Writable` where `T: PartialEq`, since a value function takes a bare one as under `Differs`. `EachApi::map` is a provided method. `RecordOp` is the function under a name over the unit's own two lists, and its old shape (`In`, `Out`, `apply(x)`) is deleted. The scalar types that had a `ResourceFootprint` impl have a `ResourceRead` impl by value from the same macro.

Departures, each forced by the language:

- `WriteVals<W>` is the shared crate's `Vals<W>`. `ReadVals<'a, R>` is the api's own product (`RCons`, `RNil`, `ReadAt`), because the shared crate's product holds `'static` values and a read may borrow: a resource read by reference, a `Window` on a column. It carries `FIXME(e86353ea1157)` and moves to the shared crate once that crate's product can hold a borrowed value.
- `ResourceRead` gains `fn read<'a>(&'a self) -> Self::Val<'a>` and the bound `Val<'a>: Copy`, and `Readable::Val<'a>` is `Copy`. Without the method a runner cannot form the value from the resource it holds, and without `Copy` the product cannot hand a member's value out of `get` (ticket 76a880867ba4).
- `EachApi::map` carries `Self: ResolveMap<R, W>`, a bridge beside the other `Resolve*` bridges. A runner cannot implement `map` for every `R: ReadSet, W: WriteSet` from those bounds alone: gathering and storing are per member, and the walk over the lists is the runner's (ticket 76a880867ba4).

## CHANGE: the positional accessors follow the member
Files: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src/write_at.rs` (new), `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/src/context.rs`.
`read`, `resource` and `fire` are bounded on `Holds`. `write` reaches a `Column<T>` the unit lists bare, under `Written` or under `Differs`: `WritesColumn<T, K>` finds the column in the write list and carries the member's rule, and `WriteAt<T, I>` holds for the list and the context together so that the accessor keeps its one inferred index. Under `Differs` the value is compared with `resolve_held`, the new second method of `ResolveColumnWrite`, and an equal value is neither stored nor counted. A bare column by position is taken as under `Written` and carries `FIXME(29cdc662ca14)`.

`ResolveAccumAppend` is an `unsafe trait` with safe methods, and `append` and `len` are safe and bounded on `Holds<Accum<T>>`. `ResolveAccumRead` is the second `unsafe trait`, with `resolve_each`, and `AccumReaderApi` with its accessor trait `HasAccumReader` (`appended()`) reads through it. `WorkUnit::Ctx` does not ask for it.

`write`, `append`, `len`, `each` on `AccumReaderApi` and `map` are provided methods of the api that call the bridges, as the design states ("The bridges are implemented by a runner's context and called by this crate's accessors"). A runner implements the bridge and names the accessor trait with an empty impl.

## CHANGE: the derive writes `ResourceRead`
Files: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api-macros/src/lib.rs`, `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api-macros/tests/derive_resource_footprint.rs`.
`#[derive(ResourceFootprint)]` also writes `ResourceRead`, from the same fields: by reference where a field's type ends in `Seq` or `Map`, by value otherwise.

A break, recorded with its reason. Read by value asks `Copy` of the value type, and the derive puts `Self: Copy` on the impl it writes, so a value type of scalar fields that derives `ResourceFootprint` and is not `Copy` no longer builds and is refused by name. The design reads such a resource by value, and a value cannot be handed out of a shared borrow without `Copy`. The one fixture in the tree that this reached, `ScalarOnly`, gains `Clone, Copy`.

## CHANGE: tests of the api and the derive
Files: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-api/tests/access_set.rs`, `contains.rs`, `store_types.rs`, `providers.rs`, `work_unit.rs`, `value_function.rs` (new), `positional_write.rs` (new), `trybuild.rs`, `tests/trybuild/*.rs` and `.stderr` (seven new pairs), the three doctests on `Window`, and the derive's test file.

- `access_set.rs` counts lists of every store kind and of wrappers, and holds `Member::Store` equal to the store each member stands for, `Written` over a type with no comparison included.
- `contains.rs` holds `Holds` at every place of a list of six and tells the two buffer modes apart, and holds `HoldsAll` over bare markers, over wrappers resolved to their stores, and over a store named twice.
- `value_function.rs` runs `map` on a real `EachApi`: a runner in the test gathers the read product, applies the function and stores what it returns. It covers a resource read by value and one read by reference, a `Window` at both ends of a column, a column written, an accumulator value appended or not, a virtual fired or not, and the empty write list. A function that returns its read unchanged is the case that fails the first test.
- `positional_write.rs` writes by position under each of the three members, at the head and past it, and counts the stores the bridge was asked for: every value under a bare column and under `Written`, only a differing one under `Differs`. `Written` over a type with no comparison is written.
- The compile-fail pairs pin the api's own messages: `Differs` over an incomparable type, `HoldsAll` naming the missing store, the positional `read` and `write` refused on a buffered column, a bare incomparable column refused in a value function's write list, an accumulator refused in its read list, and a read of a member the unit did not declare.
- `work_unit.rs` applies a `RecordOp` to a read product. The derive's tests hold `ResourceRead` by value for a struct of scalars and by reference for one with a `Seq` and a `Map`.

`store_types.rs` named the forbidden `generic_const_exprs` gate and used nothing of it. The line is deleted.

Verifications, run at this changelist:
- `cargo test -p hilavitkutin-api`: every binary green, the eight compile-fail fixtures and five doctests included.
- `cargo test -p hilavitkutin-api-macros`: 4 passed.

## CHANGE: the engine walks members and runs the value function
Files: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin/Cargo.toml`, `src/lib.rs`, `src/dispatch/engine_ctx/` (`bundles.rs`, `col_accum_project.rs`, `col_select.rs` (new), `ctx_construct.rs`, `ctx_meta_resource.rs`, `ctx_writers.rs`, `lists.rs`, `map.rs` (new), `mod.rs`, `projection.rs`, `selectors.rs`), `src/dispatch/fusion.rs`, `src/plan/project.rs`, `src/resource/bindings/` (`close.rs` (new), `column.rs` (new), `mod.rs`, `per_core.rs`, `refusal.rs`), `src/scheduler/builder.rs`, `src/scheduler/mod.rs`, `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-providers/src/adapt_wu.rs`.
The folds and projections walk a column member through `ColMember`, so a `Reach`, a `Written` and a `Differs` land on the column they name, and `MaskProject` sets the bit of `Member::Store`. `build` proves the lists with `HoldsAll`. `map` runs on `EngineCtx` as two walks in step with the projected bundles, `Gather` over the read list and `Scatter` over the write list. An accumulator in a read list projects a snapshot of its populated prefix, and the reader over it exists on a maintenance context alone.

A buffered column is one binding with a frozen and a live side, kept by `ColKind` on `Snapshot` and on `Exchange`, and it is reached through the value function alone. `BringIn` is the walk over the bindings that brings a marked live side in.

The fused entry folds a `Link`, a unit whose value function reads one `Column` and writes one. A unit that also appends is refused there at compile time (`tests/ui/appending_unit_fused_fail.rs` with its passing twin). `tests/fused_append.rs` recorded that case as a gap and is deleted with it.

Departures.

- The engine takes `notko-hlist` as a dependency and the `adt_const_params` gate, since `Reach<C, const N: Cap>` is named in its bounds. The gate is on the allowed list.
- `Link` is a trait of the engine the design does not name. The fused fold has to ask "one column in, one column out" of a unit's two lists, and a bound on the lists themselves could not say it (ticket 76a880867ba4).
- A buffered column asks the column storage for two storage ids, the live side and the frozen one, one after the other. The design states two reservations under one store id, and `ColumnStorage` reserves by id. The store keeps one position in every access mask (ticket f337323c33e9).
- The copy that `Snapshot` makes at the boundary is of the whole column and carries `FIXME(8e2e2326d588)`.

## CHANGE: the plan orders by rank, cuts a phase at a reach, and leaves a buffered column out of the wait
Files: `src/plan/edge.rs` (new), `src/plan/member.rs` (new), `src/plan/bundle_masks.rs` (new), `src/plan/access.rs`, `src/plan/grouping.rs`, `src/plan/inputs.rs`, `src/plan/mod.rs`, `src/plan/project.rs`, `src/plan/steps/step1_dag.rs`, `src/dispatch/order.rs`.
The edge rule stands in one place. `with_the_ranks` holds where the writer's rank is not past the reader's, and `raw_edge` holds where the ranks agree and the writes overlap what the reader waits on. `PlanMember` gives each member two constants, whether a read of it waits on its writer and whether it reads with a reach, and `PlanInputs` carries `frozen` (the buffered columns a unit reads) and `ranks` beside the masks. Step 1 builds a read-after-write edge on the reads less the frozen ones, so a reader and a writer of a buffered column take no edge in either registration sequence. The const grouping and the carrier masks of `dispatch/order.rs` read the same projection, `project_waits`, so the plan built at run time and the phases worked out in const agree.

A new phase opens at a unit that reads a plain column with a reach behind an earlier writer of it (`reach_behind_a_writer`), unless a waist closed the phase just before. `reach_reads_write_nothing` answers the build rule in const: no unit writes a plain column it reads with a reach.

`BundleMasks` and `UnitAccess` moved out of `grouping.rs` into `bundle_masks.rs`, with one macro for the two list shapes, and `grouping.rs` re-exports them. The split keeps both files under the size limit and changes no item.

Departures.

- `PlanMember`, `PlanInputs.frozen` and `PlanInputs.ranks`, and the items of `edge.rs`, are names the design does not carry. The design states the rules they hold.
- Two writers of one store are ordered by registration, and only where the ranks agree. The design orders them by hint priority first, then by the units downstream, and the index last. The edge carries `FIXME(hilavitkutin_orders_two_writers_of_one_store_by_registration)`.
- A read-after-write edge across two ranks that agrees with the ranks is built and stays under the registration back-edge check, so a unit of a later rank registered before the writer it reads is still refused as a back edge.

## CHANGE: the reach rule and the store positions are settled at build
Files: `src/scheduler/buildable.rs` (new), `src/scheduler/reach_rule.rs` (new), `src/scheduler/builder.rs`, `src/scheduler/mod.rs`, `src/resource/bindings/column.rs`, `src/resource/bindings/mod.rs`, `src/resource/bindings/close.rs`, `src/resource/bindings/per_core.rs`, `tests/scheduler_builder.rs`.
`ReachRule::HOLDS` is a const assertion over `reach_reads_write_nothing`, in the api's words, and `build` forces it. Its failing twin and the passing one are doctests on `ReachRule`, since a const assertion fails at monomorphisation and `cargo check` does not report it.

A column binding carries `pos`, its position in `Stores`, set at build by `PlaceIn`. `BringIn` reports the positions of the stores it brought in, and `BindingsFor::Bindings` is bounded on `BringIn`, so no run entry gained a bound.

Departures.

- `Buildable<Stores, Bindings, Witness>` is a trait of the engine the design does not name. `build` needs two witness lists, the per-unit projection and the per-column position, and a second inferred parameter on `build` and `build_with` would have changed `build_with::<DefaultRunCfg<Assert>, _, _>`, which the api design names. The one impl takes the two lists as a pair, in the trait's parameter and not in `Self`, and the entries keep their parameter count (ticket 76a880867ba4).
- A column that stands twice in `Stores` no longer builds: the position has two answers and the witness is not inferred. `mixed_kit_and_raw_build` registered a column its kit already owns, and now registers one of its own. A kit's owned stores enter `Stores` with no value and no binding, which the same ticket carries (ac91dfb44d6e).

## CHANGE: what a pass dispatches follows what the boundary brought in
Files: `src/plan/edge.rs`, `src/plan/mod.rs`, `src/scheduler/dirty.rs`, `src/scheduler/defaults.rs`, `src/scheduler/run.rs`, `src/scheduler/run_fused.rs`, `src/scheduler/run_parallel.rs`.
The boundary of `run`, `run_fused` and `run_parallel` calls `bring_in_changes`, which brings every marked live side in and leaves the positions of the stores it brought in as the next pass's seed, where it used to clear the seed. `BandWrites` holds what the units of the plan band and of the band between it and the consumers write, with every buffered column taken out, and `dirty_units` unions it into a local seed for the pass. The seed kept between passes and the count of stores seen changed are not touched by it.

Departures.

- `BandWrites` is a name the design does not carry.
- A write of a plain column from the leading band seeds its readers in the same pass and, by the mark it leaves, once more in the next. The second dispatch is one pass of work the design does not ask for and is kept, since taking it out needs the mark to tell a band's write from a maintenance write.
- `run_parallel` dispatches every unit and never asks `dirty_units`, as before this branch. The boundary still brings the changes in there.
