## mock/crates/hilavitkutin-str/DESIGN.md.tmpl

- The `str_const!` snippet builds its entry with `StaticStrEntry::new`, a `const fn`, and returns that entry's `hash`. It is the one place an entry's hash is derived from its string.
- A section "The section anchor": the Linux linker defines the section's start and stop symbols only when the link holds an input in it, so the crate places one entry itself, the empty string, and the walker returns the empty slice for a section that does not contain it. The empty string is then a const string like any other. Whether strings are collected through linker sections at all is left as the designer's open question.

## mock/crates/hilavitkutin-linking/DESIGN.md.tmpl

- The Shape paragraph: `RTLD_LOCAL` is 4 on macOS, iOS, tvOS and watchOS and 0 on every other unix, where 4 is `RTLD_NOLOAD` on glibc. It says what the wrong value did, and that the loader tests load a library the process has not mapped.

## mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/DESIGN.md.tmpl

- The origins section: `Generator::single` is offered on every shape whose `Origin` is `OneOrigin`, `Standard` and `WideKind` among them, with its totality checked per shape when it is instantiated. `Generator::at` returns `Maybe<Self>` and `Maybe::Isnt` is its answer for an out-of-range origin index.

## mock/crates/hilavitkutin-persistence/DESIGN.md.tmpl

- The content key is the full 64-bit FNV-1a hash of a string's bytes, archived as `u64`. The 28-bit statements are replaced, with the collision figures at ten thousand strings for both widths and the test that counts them.
- Evict resolves a handle to bytes through the interner and returns `PersistenceError::Missing` for one it cannot resolve. Inject accepts a const candidate only if the string it resolves to hashes back to the whole key.
- `ContentHash` is an alias for `Bits<64, Hot>`, in the paragraph and in the alias list and the key-types table.
- The arvo pin: `arvo-hash` and `arvo-refit` are read at `1b2796e9`, a revision arvo's `dev` no longer carries.

## mock/crates/hilavitkutin-extensions-macros/DESIGN.md.tmpl

- A section "Trampoline survival under fat LTO": one reader of object files on every host, the three conditions (descriptor exported, trampoline names in the symbol table where there is one, init and shutdown pointers present and distinct), and what stands in for the second on a PE image.

## mock/crates/hilavitkutin-extensions-macros/BACKLOG.md.tmpl

- The "LTO smoke on Windows" entry goes, since the design now carries it. The runtime-callability entry no longer says the smoke reads `nm` output.

## mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl

- The EMA vectorisation sentence no longer claims NEON wins 4 to 9 times over autovec. It says no speedup is claimed for the vector arms yet, and gives what the one bench measured: an accumulator carried across one stream where the shipped contract is an independent element-wise update, NEON 41 ns against 85 ns for scalar (2.07 times) at N = 1024, within 1.4 percent end to end, and an autovec arm that is no compiler baseline.
