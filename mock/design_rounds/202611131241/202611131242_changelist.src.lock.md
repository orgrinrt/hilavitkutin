## CHANGE: the plan band is entered on the first frame or on a plan-dirty bit
Files: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin/src/scheduler/mod.rs`, `builder.rs`, `defaults.rs`, `dirty.rs`, `run.rs`, `run_fused.rs`, `run_parallel.rs`, `worker.rs`.
From round `202607200900`, clause S2. The scheduler carries `plan_band: AtomicBool`. One function, `consume_plan_dirty`, clears the plan-dirty bits, decides the band and publishes the decision; `run` and `run_parallel` both call it at the top of a frame, where each spelled the same block before. The leading band and each worker read the published decision. `__plan_band` is the hidden accessor the tests read. `run_fused` has no plan band and does not consume the bits, so a plan-affecting swap before a fused frame stands until the next unfused one; whether that is intended is not specified and carries `FIXME(b8637199eb8a)`.
