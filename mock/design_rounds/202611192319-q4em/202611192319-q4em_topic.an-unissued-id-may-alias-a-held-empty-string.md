# Topic: an id the arena did not issue may resolve to a held empty string

**Date:** 2026-11-19
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** `hilavitkutin-str` (`ArenaInterner` contract), `hilavitkutin-providers` (`MemoryArena` rustdoc, `tests/interner_law.rs`), the hilavitkutin ticket row `evict_str_turns_an_unresolvable_runtime_str_into_the_hash_of`
**Source topics:** the third and last pr-reviewer-senior pass over `work/hila-consumer-surface` at `0c1fadef`, blocker 1 and finding 3; round `202611192301-pt7z`, closed, whose wording this narrows

## What the record says

Round `202611192301-pt7z` wrote that `arena_resolve` of an id at or past the live count reads nothing outside the arena's storage "and gives no string the arena holds", in the `ArenaInterner` contract and in the `MemoryArena` rustdoc. The green tests that check it never put `""` among the held strings.

## Where the record is wrong

`MemoryArena` answers every unissued id with `""`, and `""` is a held string once anything interns it through the arena: always through `ArenaInterner`, and through `StringInterner` on a target where `""` is not const. The reviewer showed it on `MemoryArena<16, 4>` and on `MemoryArena<0, 4>`. The claim is one the code does not keep.

## Decision for this round

Derived, not asked. The contract keeps only the part that holds: no read outside the arena's storage. What an unissued id resolves to stays out of the contract, and the design says plainly that it may equal a held empty string, the gap the ticket already tracks. The green tests are named for what they check, no held non-empty string. A red interns `""` through `ArenaInterner` and asks that an unissued id not alias it, under the same ticket; the ticket row lists its reds by the ids they carry.
