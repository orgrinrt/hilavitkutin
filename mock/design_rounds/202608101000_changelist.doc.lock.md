# Doc changelist: a shape says which minter, not where it starts

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Phase:** DOC
**Topic:** `202608101000_topic.disjointness-by-construction-not-by-law.md`

## CHANGE: the origin section describes an index, not a pair of bounds

File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

The document says origins divide the id space so two minters cannot collide
however far either counts. The code let a shape supply two arbitrary numbers, and
three rounds of numbers were wrong: first placed past the field, then placed
inside it but overlapping.

The document now says what the mechanism is. A shape declares **which minter an
origin is**; the run it owns is derived, `[index << COUNTER_BITS, ((index+1) <<
COUNTER_BITS) - 1]`. Distinct indices own disjoint runs by arithmetic, so an
overlapping shape cannot be written rather than being caught by a law.

It also records why the previous answer was wrong in kind rather than in detail:
**both laws were written to reject the construction that had just been found**,
each succeeded at that, and neither described the property. A law samples the
shapes that exist; a derivation covers the ones that do not yet.

## CHANGE: the coverage section, on what a law is for after this

File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

What remains testable about origins is the shape's own mapping: distinct origins
map to distinct indices, and an index fits `ORIGIN_BITS`. The section says so, and
says that the fit law is now a consequence of the sum law rather than a separate
assertion.

## CHANGE: the public overview goes back to being generated

Files: `docs/HILAVITKUTIN_SYM_OVERVIEW.md`, `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

Round nine's header claims no template sits behind the overview and nothing
regenerates it. **Both halves are false.** It is rendered from `DESIGN.md.tmpl`
under a name the current mockspace no longer emits, which is why a generator run
would delete rather than update it, and why no same-named template exists to find.

The claim was inferred from that absence rather than tested. The banner round
nine removed was correct; the filename was the stale part.

Every claim the hand-written file carries that the template does not is moved
into the template, and the hand-written file is left for the rename migration
that owns it rather than being hand-patched a third time.
