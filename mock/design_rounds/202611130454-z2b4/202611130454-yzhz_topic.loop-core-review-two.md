# loop-core-review-two

This round answers the second review of the loop core on the same branch, `work/loop-core`, taken at `6e4c2ff` after round `202611130355-yrri` closed. The review found the nine findings and the five shapes fixed in that round addressed, and the threaded runner proof sound. It asks for two small corrections and four records before the branch merges.

The repository declares no canon, so the designs govern, and the plan they answer is still roadmap step HL0a of the engine roadmap r7.

## What the review asks

One role per column holds only while the places `P` are inferred. The design and the walk's module text said a role list naming one column twice does not build, but the declaration builds and only `bind` fails, by ambiguity, so a caller who writes `P` out can put two members of one column on two roles. The statement is corrected to what holds, and the explicit case is catalogued as an ignored test that should fail to compile, with its catalogue row and a marker citing its ticket, unless the places can be kept from the caller cheaply.

`Unit::gather`, `absorb` and `words` accepted any view of the domain, so a runner could apply one table's handles to another store's committed view, with wrong values or a bounds panic. The view is to come from the checked table or the units, or the parameter restricted to the store's own view, or the gap recorded with a ticket.

Two shapes join the list of those not locked: `P` in every runner signature, which carries `notko-hlist`'s place encoding into the plumbing, and `Value` sealed with `Int` its one inhabitant, which leaves downstream content with no value type until a `FormatId` uniqueness rule exists. `Stage::take`'s contract says outright that taking marks a column written whether or not anything is put to it. And the reference crate's dev-dependencies on the unpublished frozen crates are checked against `cargo publish`.

## Not decided here

How the places are kept from a caller, which value types a consumer may add, and anything of roadmap step HL0b.
