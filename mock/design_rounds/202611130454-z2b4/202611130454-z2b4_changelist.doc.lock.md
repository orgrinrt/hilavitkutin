This changelist corrects one statement in the contracts design, moves the committed view into the unit a runner is handed, and records two more shapes as not locked. Nothing outside the contracts and reference designs changes, beside the workspace manifest and the catalogue.

## CHANGE: one role per column holds while the places are inferred

**Files:** `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-contracts/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, `mock/catalogue.md`

The design said a role list naming a column twice does not build. The declaration builds, and its members then have two places each: where the places are inferred, the bind is ambiguous (E0283) and does not build, but a caller who writes `P` out can place the read of a column on one role and its write on another, and the bind passes. The design now says so. The case is catalogued as an ignored compile-fail test in the reference crate's tests, `tests/ui/catalogue/explicit_places.rs`, with the row `hilavitkutin_catalogue_explicit_places_bind_one_column_s_mem` and a marker citing ticket `64c5363cc578` where the roles are walked. Closing it cheaply is not possible this pass: any place encoding a caller can spell lets it pick the second place of a duplicate column, and refusing duplicates in `Roles` itself needs negative reasoning the stack does not use.

## CHANGE: a unit carries its store's committed view

**Files:** `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-contracts/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-reference/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

`Unit` becomes `Unit<'v, K, R>`, the unit's handles paired with `R`, the committed view of the store its table was checked against, with no public field and no public constructor. `gather`, `absorb` and `words` take a position and no view, and `Unit::extent` says how many records the view holds. Only `Bound::each`, which takes the view from the store it holds, and `Units::next`, which takes it from the step, make one. `UnitFn` gains the view type `R` and takes the unit's lifetime per call; `StagedFn` gains `R`; `Staging` carries the unit and so the view; `Units` carries the step's committed view and says its `extent`; `StepFn::step` takes the units alone. The handles and the walk that mints them become crate-private, so nothing outside the crate can pair handles with another view. The reference runner and the threaded runner proof hold no view of their own.

## CHANGE: taking a part marks its columns written

**Files:** `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-contracts/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

`Stage::take` marks the columns it splits off as written whether or not anything is then put to them, so a runner that takes a part and writes nothing commits whatever its staged cells held. The design and the trait's contract say this outright.
