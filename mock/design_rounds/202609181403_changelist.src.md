## CHANGE: tautological tests FROM present TO deleted

A test is tautological here when its assertion can fail only if the literal body it restates changes: a constructor's defaults, a field written then read, a derived `PartialEq` or `Default`, or a stub returning a constant. None of them derives anything from its inputs.

Files deleted whole: `hilavitkutin-persistence/tests/cold_store.rs` (five tests restating the skeleton bodies of `open`, `flush`, `snapshot` and `load`), `hilavitkutin-persistence/tests/manifest.rs` (a constant compared with its own literal, field write-then-read, `Default`), `hilavitkutin/tests/core_assignment.rs` (`new()` and field writes), `hilavitkutin/tests/plan_inputs.rs` (`new()`, `Default` against `new()`, field writes; `AccessMask` set and contains stay covered in `plan_types.rs`).
