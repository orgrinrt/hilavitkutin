## CHANGE: tautological tests FROM present TO deleted

A test is tautological here when its assertion can fail only if the literal body it restates changes: a constructor's defaults, a field written then read, a derived `PartialEq` or `Default`, or a stub returning a constant. None of them derives anything from its inputs.

## CHANGE: threaded tests and the engine bench FROM `OsThreadPool` TO their own executors

Files: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin/tests/common/mod.rs` (new), `mock/crates/hilavitkutin/tests/test_executor.rs` (new), the nine threaded test files that named `OsThreadPool`, `mock/benches/engine_vs_std/src/executor.rs` (new), its `lib.rs`, `accumulator.rs`, `wide_parallel.rs`.

`TestExecutor` spawns a `std::thread` per `spawn` and asserts, before spawning, that the closure fits one pointer-sized, pointer-aligned slot, which is what a no-alloc executor needs to hand it to an OS thread without boxing; every threaded test now holds the scheduler to that. `test_executor.rs` pins the check both ways: a captureless closure and a one-pointer capture fit, a two-pointer capture and a 32-byte-aligned capture do not. It also pins the executor itself: `spawn` runs the closure on a thread other than the caller's, refuses a two-pointer closure with a panic at the call site (with the assert disabled that test fails), and `worker_count` is the host's `available_parallelism`. The bench's `BenchExecutor` is std threads too; its workers spawn once, outside the timed region, so the timed body is unchanged, but the bench's executor is no longer pthread-direct.

Verification: `cargo test -p hilavitkutin` passes, `test_executor` included; `cargo check --all-targets` in `mock/benches/engine_vs_std` succeeds.

## CHANGE: rustfmt over every touched file, and each `lint:allow` back on its line

Files: the source files this round touched.

The commit hook runs rustfmt on staged files, and rustfmt moves a comment trailing an item it reflows, so a `// lint:allow(...)` could land on a line of its own, away from the line it suppresses. Each was put back on that line. Where rustfmt would move it again, the code is reshaped so it does not: a literal bound in a `let` of its own, a parameter on its own line, a const in an `impl` block of its own. Two shapes cannot be reshaped and carry `#[rustfmt::skip]`: an item whose allow trails a `{` or a `where` (rustfmt moves it into the body), and an impl whose associated items rustfmt reorders (it moves every trailing comment but the last onto the next item). The reordering had already shifted the reasons of the `PlanDims` impls one item down; each reason is back on its own item.

Verification: `grep -rnE '^\s*// lint:allow' mock/crates mock/benches --include='*.rs'` returns nothing (`fdb6177` had one such line, in `hilavitkutin-providers/src/storage.rs`), and running the repo's `rustfmt.toml` over a copy of every touched file leaves the set of lines carrying a `lint:allow` unchanged.
