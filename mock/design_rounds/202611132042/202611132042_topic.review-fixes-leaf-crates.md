# review-fixes-leaf-crates

Round topic for the fixes the first review of `work/hv-leaf` (tip `7dc08813`) asked for. The report is `mock/research/202611132042_review-hv-leaf/REVIEW.md`. It returned merge after fixes with eight findings. Findings 1, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 are source only. Findings 2 and 3 change design text.

## Scope

- `hilavitkutin-linking`: both dlopen flags come from `libc` per target instead of from constants written here (finding 2). On 32-bit Android `RTLD_NOW` is 0, and `RTLD_LOCAL` is 0x200 on NetBSD and QNX and 0x80000 on AIX (`libc` 0.2.191, `src/unix/linux_like/android/b32/mod.rs:178`, `bsd/netbsdlike/netbsd/mod.rs:1462`, `nto/mod.rs:1048`, `aix/mod.rs:590`). The design sentence that says 0 on every other unix is corrected.
- `hilavitkutin-sym`: the design says a one-origin shape with its origin out of range does not compile with `single` in use; the code asserts in the body of the const fn (finding 3). The design sentence is brought to the code, and a `should_panic` test covers the failure path.
- `hilavitkutin-persistence`: the ten-thousand-string test goes through `evict_str` and shows a width that collides; the design's "counts at the stored width" is brought to what the test does (finding 1). The ambiguity of a const handle that two registered strings share is catalogued as a ticket (finding 5).
- `hilavitkutin-str`: the tautology in `an_entry_hash_is_the_hash_of_its_string` is replaced by the derivation (finding 4).
- `hilavitkutin-persistence/src/string_table.rs`: the lint allowance goes back onto the signature it justifies (finding 6).
- `hilavitkutin-extensions-macros`: the fixture comment that still names `nm`, and the survival check counting undefined symbols (finding 7).
- `hilavitkutin-linking/tests/not_yet_loaded.rs`: the candidate libraries are searched in the common library directories (finding 8).
