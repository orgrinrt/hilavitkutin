## mock/crates/hilavitkutin-linking/DESIGN.md.tmpl

- The Shape paragraph: both dlopen flags take their values from the `libc` crate for the target. `RTLD_LOCAL` is 4 on the Apple targets, 0 on Linux, 0x200 on NetBSD and QNX and 0x80000 on AIX, and `RTLD_NOW` is 0 on 32-bit Android. The earlier sentence, 0 on every other unix, was wrong and is replaced.

## mock/crates/hilavitkutin-sym/DESIGN.md.tmpl

- The origins section: the range check of `Generator::single` is an assertion in the constructor, a build error where `single` sits in a constant and a panic at the first call elsewhere, `Default` included. It says why the check is not made at instantiation, and that a test pins the panic. The earlier sentence, that such a shape does not compile with `single` in use, is replaced.

## mock/crates/hilavitkutin-persistence/DESIGN.md.tmpl

- The paragraph on the key width: the ten-thousand-string test goes through `evict_str` and counts again at a narrow width, and a second test searches for two strings sharing their low 28 bits and requires them keyed apart. The earlier sentence, that the test counts collisions at the stored width, is replaced.
