# Topic: revise the api in place

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Round:** 202611130617-h6mb
**Scope:** `hilavitkutin-api` and its design, the engine's design where the api's change reaches it, the root design
**Source:** the lead designer's words of 12 and 13 November 2026 as the job brief `hv-api-revision` records them, and the map `briefs/fixup/map-loop-core.md` of the kaski channel

The branch `work/loop-core` (`5603f85`) built what kaski's engine needs as five crates beside the existing scheduler, with a vocabulary of their own, and its design text called `hilavitkutin-api` the legacy contract. The lead designer held it and had it mapped against his own design. The map found 52 items, of which 20 are an existing concept under a second name, 7 extend one, 3 correct one, 17 are new and belong here, and 5 do not belong in hilavitkutin at all. He then chose to have the new and extending items enter `hilavitkutin-api` and its design in place.

This file records his words, what governs the round, and the decisions that are about the round as a whole: what it takes from the map, what it leaves alone, the adoption of the shared list, formats, registration, and what becomes of the flat topics already open. The shape of the value form, the domains and the frame-buffered kind is the second topic of this round, `202611130617-h6mb_topic.value-form-domains-and-the-buffered-kind.md`.

No human sat in this round. Every decision below is the helper seat's, taken from his words, the designs and the map, and each is provisional until he reads it. Where neither his words nor a design decides, nothing is decided here and a question ticket is named instead.

## What governs

hilavitkutin declares no canon (`mockspace.toml` has no `canon_paths`), so the designs stand in: `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl` and each crate's `DESIGN.md.tmpl`. A design line is changed only by this round's doc changelist naming it, quoting it and giving the reason, and only where the map classed the item as an extension, a correction or new. The map, the branch and the brief are claims; the round tested the central ones in a sketch before writing a changelist.

One rule of this repository reads against the round and is set aside on his word. The mutation order says that changing a design nukes the code under it. He asked for the api revised in place, and the brief asks that the planner, fusion, morsels, kits and the rest keep working with their suites green. So the code under the changed design lines is revised and not deleted. The doc changelist states this as a departure.

## What the round takes from the map

By the map's row numbers.

Extensions and corrections that enter the api: row 1 (the value form is `RecordOp` widened to lists, and becomes the form an author writes first), row 9 (a declared neighbour read, as a member of the read list), row 21 (two writers of a frame-buffered store are refused, the priority rule standing for every other kind), row 25 (a write is safe because a type the plumbing mints carries the exclusivity), row 27 (the committed side of a frame-buffered store is to columns over a pass what the resource snapshot is to resources over a phase), row 38 (the one shared list in place of the api's copy).

New things that enter the api: row 4 (a record domain, as a parameter of a column and a record count per domain in the plan), rows 5 and 12 (an offset in a domain, and the value past the extent declared at the read), row 30 (the frame-buffered kind, a sibling of `Column` and `Accum`).

Left out on purpose. Row 2 (an id declared by the author): identity stays the type. Rows 14 to 16 (a sealed value type, a universal cell, a format tag): any `Copy` type stays a column at its native stride. Row 13 (a role list written by the author): the union of the lists is computed. Row 33 (a visiting order written by an implementor): the sequence of records is the engine's. Row 10 (one resource value per unit of the same type): not asked for by the brief, and a unit generic over its resource marker already says it, so nothing is added. Row 17 (a data-bound table): not in the contract, see below. Rows 41 to 52 (the digest, the oracle, the laws, the frozen crates, the ledger, the lints, the cross-target tool) are the next round.

## The shared list

Options weighed: keep the api's own `Cons`, `Empty`, `Contains`, `ContainsAll`, `Concat` and add what the value form needs beside them; or take `notko-hlist` for all of it.

Taken: `notko-hlist`, whole. The flat topic `202607281547_topic.adopt-the-shared-hlist.md` already argues it, the map's row 38 calls the copy a fault, and the value form needs the shared crate's product of values and its position witness anyway, so keeping the copy would mean two lists in one crate. `AccessSet` keeps its name as the api's own bound over the shared list, its count read from the shared crate's cardinal, and the api's diagnostics on a missing store are kept, as that topic suggests. The api re-exports the list names an author writes, so an author's crate still names one crate.

## Formats

Nothing changes, and the design says so in one sentence where a reader would look for it. `ColumnValue` stays the blanket over `Copy + 'static`. The new kinds take any `ColumnValue`. No sealed value type, no cell trait, no format tag and no author-declared id enters the contract.

## Registration

Typed registration stays the only registration in the contract. He asked to decide on a data-bound table "in the api round", with a sketch and a bench of both. The round brings him that: a sketch under `mock/research/sketches/` of a table bound from data over the erased, shape-described addressing he chose for resources on 2 July (`mock/design_rounds/202607161000/202606210600_topic.hybrid-decision.md`), a bench under `mock/benches/` with a typed arm and a data-bound arm over a column workload, and a question ticket with the numbers and the options. The design is not changed for it in this round. The storage bench he remembers measured the erased form at parity for resources and holds no number for columns, which is why the bench is owed before the question.

## What does not change

Read-after-write edges between units, the planner's steps, phases and trunks and fibers, morsel sizing, the memory provider and `ColumnStorage`, resources and their snapshot, virtuals and `On<V>`, accumulators, scheduling hints, `Plannable`, kits, persistence, the context framework and the extension layer all stay. The doc changelist goes through the map's section 2 list item by item and says for each what the revision changes for it or that it changes nothing.

## Returned to while writing the doc changelist

Two things in "The shared list" above did not hold as written.

The count. `AccessSet`'s count cannot be read from the shared crate's cardinal by this repository: the shared crate counts through `Cardinal`, the count here is arvo's `USize`, and neither is hilavitkutin's, so the implementation of one for the other is arvo's to write. The api keeps its own recursion over the shared types, and the idea is ticketed against arvo.

The diagnostics. Keeping the api's messages needs a mechanism, since an attribute cannot be put on the shared crate's trait. The api declares its own bound, implemented by one blanket over the shared membership and marked so the compiler reports it and not the shared trait. A fixture added to the round's sketch records the resulting message.

The parked topic's sketch of alias names for the cell and the end of an access list is not decided by this round either. It is his sketch and says itself that the leaf's name is open, so it went to him as a question ticket and the shared names are re-exported meanwhile.

## Found on the way, and not this round's to change

Three things in the designs disagree with the source or with the workspace's rules and are outside what the map classed, so the round does not rewrite them and files a ticket for each: the first sections of the api's design name `Provider`, `ProviderKind` and `LinkedBin` where the megaround left `BuilderInput` and `ExtensionSurface`; the engine crate enables `generic_const_exprs`, which the workspace forbids, and both designs name it; and the api's design says planning reads `Plannable` while the engine reads `WorkUnit`.
