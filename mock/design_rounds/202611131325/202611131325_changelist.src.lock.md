## CHANGE: `tests/a4_parallel_fiber_windows.rs` reads `Narrow` and `Wide`

The attributes on `Narrow` and `Wide` go. The test poisoned both columns through `*mut u32` and `*mut u64` casts and read them back the same way, so the record types it declares were never what it looked at. It now takes `*mut Narrow` and `*mut Wide` from the bindings, poisons with `Narrow(u32::MAX)` and `Wide(u64::MAX)`, and reads each record's field.

## CHANGE: `tests/gate2_accumulator.rs` reads the side column

The attribute on `Sv` goes. `SideWu` wrote `In[i] + 1` into its column and nothing looked at it. `accumulator_in_a_two_trunk_phase_matches_single_core` now poisons the side column with `Sv(u32::MAX)` beside the other two and, after the frame, asserts every record holds `i + 1`. That says the second trunk is walked whole, which the probe's comment states and nothing checked.

Shown failing first: with `SideWu` skipping record 0, the test fails on `side rec 0`. The unit was put back.
