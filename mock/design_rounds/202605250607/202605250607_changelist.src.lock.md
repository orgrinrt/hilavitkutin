## CHANGE: codegen_core lint:allow retained (finding 2 partially declined)
File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin/src/dispatch/mod.rs`
Verification: `grep -A 1 'pub fn codegen_core' mock/crates/hilavitkutin/src/dispatch/mod.rs | head -2 | grep -q 'lint:allow'`.

The reviewer flagged the Round 1 drive-by `lint:allow` on the `codegen_core` signature as out-of-scope. Investigation: the annotation is load-bearing under the `mockspace-write-guard` hook. Once any edit touches `dispatch/mod.rs`, the hook re-scans the file and flags the pre-existing `const MAX_FIBERS: usize` bare-primitive at the `codegen_core` signature. The annotation cannot be removed without either (a) failing the hook, or (b) replacing the const-generic with `arvo::Cap<N>` (a substantial type-system shift across all const-generic positions in the engine, out of C2 scope). The annotation stays as the smaller of the two evils, matching the existing pattern on the adjacent `assign_cores` const-generics. A separate sweep round can either propagate the annotation to all remaining unannotated const-generics in the engine, or refactor the lot to `arvo::Cap<N>`; both are out of scope for C2.
