5. `hilavitkutin-api/src/dispatch_codegen.rs` and `src/lib.rs`: `LockFreeDispatch` and `Scheduled` are removed with their impls and re-exports, which also removes the placeholder marker the `FIXME` there tracked; `work_unit.rs` stops explaining `HasSchedule` by them.
