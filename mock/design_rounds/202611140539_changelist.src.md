9. `hilavitkutin/tests`: the ui pass twins of `unregistered_store` and `unregistered_wrapped_store` write what they read over four records and assert it, with each fail twin changed alike; the spawn-once test changes its input between frames and asserts the second frame; the run outcomes `gate2_full_dispatch`, `gate2_virtual_firing` and `fused_linear_chain` discarded are asserted; the `fixed_workers` allow names the bare const-generic parameter; the kit smokes in `scheduler_builder.rs` are named for what they check; `r5_core_idle` asserts the exact count of walking cores, with a surplus-core case; `scheduler_builder.rs` and `r5_core_idle.rs` split under 500 lines along their fixtures, into `#[path]` modules under `tests/scheduler_builder/` and `tests/r5_core_idle/`; and every column read-back through `*const u32` or `*mut u32` reads through the column's own newtype.
