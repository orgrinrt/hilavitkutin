# The widths that are still free, and a forbidden gate that does nothing

**Date:** 2026-08-09
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** `hilavitkutin-sym`, `hilavitkutin-str`

Round five tied `KIND_BITS` and `ID_BITS` to the masks their layouts actually
carry and declared the class closed. It was closed for two of the four widths a
shape declares. This round closes the other two, and removes a forbidden feature
gate that enables nothing.

## One: the split between origin and counter is free

A shape declares four widths. Two are now tied to facts: `width_of` counts
`KIND_BITS` from `KIND_MASK` and `ID_BITS` from `ID_MASK`, and no impl can
override a free function.

The remaining two are tied only to each other and to a third declaration.
`every_shape_id_is_origin_plus_counter` asserts `ORIGIN_BITS + COUNTER_BITS ==
ID_BITS`, and `every_shape_accounts_for_all_thirty_two_bits` asserts the four
sum to the handle. Both constrain the **sum**. Neither constrains the **split**.

So one may be raised and the other lowered freely. Proved rather than argued:
`SixteenMinters` was edited to declare `ORIGIN_BITS` of five over `COUNTER_BITS`
of twenty-three, leaving the sum at twenty-eight. All sixteen tests passed. Its
own `origin_base` shifts the minter index up by twenty-four and its
`origin_ceiling` masks `0x00FF_FFFF`, twenty-four bits. The declaration and the
mechanism disagreed by one bit in each direction and nothing in the crate
noticed.

This is the fourth appearance of one class, and the shape of its survival is the
part worth recording. **A law relating two declarations to each other constrains
neither.** Three rounds tied declarations to declarations, each time producing a
consistent story, which is exactly what a wrong declaration also produces.

### The law

Tie `COUNTER_BITS` to what the mechanism does, not to another declaration. For
every shape and every origin:

```
origin_ceiling(o) - origin_base(o)  ==  2^COUNTER_BITS - 1
```

That is the span a minter owns, read off the two functions that define it. A
wrong `COUNTER_BITS` fails it whatever the other declarations say. `ORIGIN_BITS`
then follows from the existing sum law, which becomes load-bearing once the
other term is pinned to a fact.

Written for every shape, not the varying one, because choosing which
instantiations to check is choosing what not to find out.

The lying split gets written, watched fail with the shape named, and deleted,
which is the only evidence that the law constrains anything.

## Two: a forbidden feature gate enabling nothing

`hilavitkutin-str/tests/handle.rs` opens with `#![feature(generic_const_exprs)]`
and `#![allow(incomplete_features)]`. That feature is forbidden outright, not
watched: `min_generic_const_args` is the sanctioned successor and the workspace
reached zero live gates on the full feature two rounds of policy ago.

It is also dead. The file has no `where [(); ...]` bound and no `cap_size`
expression. Both lines were removed and all seven tests passed.

The PR carrying this round certifies `hilavitkutin-str` clean, so the gate cannot
stay in it.

## What this round does not do

The generated `docs/` tree still disagrees with its templates, and regenerating
renames every overview document under a newer convention. That is a migration and
it does not belong in a widths round.
