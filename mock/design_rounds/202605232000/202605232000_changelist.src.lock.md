## CHANGE: `Scheduler::plan_dirty` typestate lift deferred per rustc generic_const_exprs limitation

File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin/src/scheduler/mod.rs`
Topic A2 pivot. The reviewer's recommendation to lift `Scheduler::plan_dirty` from `[AtomicBool; 256]` to `[AtomicBool; Cfg::MAX_PLAN_AFFECTING_RESOURCES.0]` runs into a rustc limitation: `generic_const_exprs` rejects field access on generic constants ("overly complex generic constant"). The block-syntax variant reproduces the same error.

The intended shape is documented in source via an extended comment + lint:allow rationale on the `plan_dirty` field. The `Scheduler::run` signature and the per-Cfg typestate of the surrounding `Scheduler<Cfg: RunCfg>` shell remain as the prior round locked them; only the per-Cfg width of the dirty bitmap awaits rustc's feature work, tracked under `#345`.

Verification: `grep 'per-Cfg lift awaits rustc generic_const_exprs gaining field-access support' mock/crates/hilavitkutin/src/scheduler/mod.rs` returns one match.

## CHANGE: `AdaptCtxPlaceholder` renamed to `AdaptCtxUnimplementedStub`

File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin-providers/src/adapt_wu.rs`
Topic A3. Rename of the `AdaptWu` Ctx placeholder (and its accompanying `Stub` provider) makes the stub status visible at the type level. Any consumer who registers `AdaptWu` and calls `Scheduler::run` hits the engine's `unimplemented!()` before the Ctx's `unimplemented!()` ever runs (dual-unreachability).
