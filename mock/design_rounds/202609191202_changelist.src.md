## CHANGE: the forcing `engine-spawn-through-gate` accepts FROM any `OnePointerClosure::<..>::FITS` TO one naming the spawned argument's type, compiled in

Files: `mock/lints/engine_spawn_through_gate.rs`, `mock/lints/engine_spawn_through_gate/tests.rs`.

The lint reads the parameter list of `fn spawn_one_pointer` off its signature, across lines up to the body brace, skipping a `(` inside the generics. A `spawn` call inside the body counts as gated only when its argument is a single identifier naming one of those parameters, and an earlier forcing in the same body names that parameter's declared type in its turbofish, whitespace ignored on both sides. A forcing with `#[cfg` (which takes in `#[cfg_attr`) earlier on its own line, or on any of the attribute-only lines directly above it, blank and comment lines between them included, does not count. A `#[cfg` on some other statement near the forcing does not affect it.

What it does not read: a spawn argument split across lines, and a turbofish on the spawn call with a nested generic, are both refused rather than matched, which is the strict side. A forcing inside a nested block of the gate body still counts, since the const is evaluated whether or not the block runs.

Six new tests, 24 in all:

- `a_forcing_that_names_another_type_than_the_spawned_one_is_refused`: `<()>`, `<u8>`, `<P>`, `<&P>`, `<Wrapper<F>>`, `<>`.
- `a_forcing_under_cfg_is_refused`: `#[cfg(any())]` above, `#[cfg(test)]` above, `#[cfg_attr(..)]` above, the cfg with an `#[allow]` between, the cfg below an `#[allow]`, the cfg with a blank and a comment line between, and `#[cfg(any())] let () = ...;` on the forcing's own line.
- `a_cfg_on_an_earlier_statement_does_not_unforce`: `#[cfg(test)]` on a statement before and after the forcing.
- `the_forcing_is_matched_by_type_not_by_name`: the gate fn written over `G` and `g` passes, and forcing `<F>` there is refused.
- `equivalent_spellings_of_the_right_forcing_pass`: `::< F >`, `spawn::<F>(f)`, `spawn( f )`, a `FnOnce() -> ()` bound in the generics with `mut f`, and the parameter list across lines.
- `a_spawn_of_something_other_than_a_parameter_is_refused`: `spawn(h)`, `spawn(move || {})`, and `spawn(pool)`, the last a parameter of the wrong type.

The earlier `a_forcing_that_does_not_bind_unit_is_refused` stays as it was; the new refused shapes sit in the two sibling tests above.

Negative controls, each run with `cargo test` in the generated lint crate, then restored and re-run green:

- Type match off (any compiled-in forcing gates the body's spawns): 3 failed, `a_forcing_that_names_another_type_than_the_spawned_one_is_refused` (first at `<()>`), `a_spawn_of_something_other_than_a_parameter_is_refused` (at `spawn(pool)`), `the_forcing_is_matched_by_type_not_by_name`.
- Parameter requirement off (a spawn of a non-parameter gated by any forcing): 1 failed, `a_spawn_of_something_other_than_a_parameter_is_refused` at `spawn(h)`.
- `#[cfg` refusal off entirely: 1 failed, `a_forcing_under_cfg_is_refused` at the first case, `#[cfg(any())]` above.
- Only the same-line half off: 1 failed, the same test at the `#[cfg(any())] let () = ...;` assertion.
