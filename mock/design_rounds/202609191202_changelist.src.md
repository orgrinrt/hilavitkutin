## CHANGE: the forcing `engine-spawn-through-gate` accepts FROM any `OnePointerClosure::<..>::FITS` TO one naming the spawned argument's type, compiled in

Files: `mock/lints/engine_spawn_through_gate.rs`, `mock/lints/engine_spawn_through_gate/tests.rs`.

The lint reads the parameter list of `fn spawn_one_pointer` off its signature. A `spawn` call inside the body counts as gated only when its argument is one of those parameters and an earlier forcing in the same body names that parameter's declared type in its turbofish. A forcing with `#[cfg` on its own line, or on the attribute lines directly above it, does not count.
