# loop-core-contracts

This round births the contracts a kernel author and a store implementor write against, the one-thread reference runner under them, the laws a run is held to, and two frozen crates, one of authors and one of implementors, that the laws run over. It is roadmap step HL0a of the engine roadmap r7 (`briefs/fixup/pl1-engine-roadmap-r7.md` in the workspace channel), which turns r6's order round for the part a consumer writes against: the contracts come first, in new crates beside the engine, so that fusion and threading land later in the plumbing and demand nothing of an implementor. The existing engine and `hilavitkutin-api` are not touched, other than `hilavitkutin-api` being marked as the legacy contract it now is.

Three sketches stand under it, all committed. `mock/research/sketches/202611122252_runner-generic-workunit/` settles the encoding (E2) and spells the two-axis neighbour as plain data in the domain. `mock/research/sketches/202611122242_frame-view-typestate/` settles the data-bound checked table and a handle typed by its value. `mock/research/sketches/202611130039_wasm-digest-under-node/` settles the digest fold and the script that compares native and wasm32 digests, which this round moves to `mock/tools/`.

This repository declares no canon (no `canon_paths` in `mockspace.toml`), so its designs govern. The doc changelist supersedes four statements of `hilavitkutin-api`'s design and of the root design for the new crates only, and adds to the root design; the existing crates keep their designs and their code, so nothing is nuked.

## The five crates

`hilavitkutin-contracts` holds what an author names and what an implementor writes: the list vocabulary, the number proxies, a domain with its columns and resources and the members of a read list, the digest, the value kernel and its identity, the bind with the checked table and the typed column handle, and the store with its visiting order. `hilavitkutin-reference` is the one-thread runner: a step, a swap and a digest, and `run` as a library function taking a checked table. `hilavitkutin-laws` holds the laws B1 to B3 as functions over plain data returning a verdict, and the ledger check of the frozen crates. `hilavitkutin-frozen-implementors` holds two stores of different layouts and two visiting orders. `hilavitkutin-frozen-authors` holds author 1, a value kernel on a two-axis domain generic over its lane, beside its model.

None of the five depends on `hilavitkutin` or `hilavitkutin-api`. The frozen crates depend on `hilavitkutin-contracts` and nothing else, and the laws crate depends on no runner.

## Calls this round takes

Every call the plan leaves to its default is taken at the default. The ones the plan left open, and what each rests on, go into the doc changelist in full; in short: a domain declares how its keys are walked and where a step lands (`first`, `next`, `reach` over a run-time shape), so a store is written once for every domain; the neighbour member is `Near<C, O>` with the offset a domain-declared type carrying its step as plain data, as the runner-generic sketch's two-axis case shows; the proxy lane's term bound is `2^BITS - 1`, one less than the plan's figure, because `2^BITS` subtracted minimums overflow the `2 * BITS` register by one; a coefficient's format mismatch is its own refusal; and B3 is stated so that it fails the mutant the plan names for it.
