## CHANGE: `Scheduler::plan_dirty` typestate lift deferred per rustc generic_const_exprs limitation

File: `mock/crates/hilavitkutin/src/scheduler/mod.rs`
Topic A2 pivot. The reviewer's recommendation to lift `plan_dirty` from `[AtomicBool; 256]` to `[AtomicBool; Cfg::MAX_PLAN_AFFECTING_RESOURCES.0]` runs into a rustc limitation: `generic_const_exprs` rejects field access on generic constants ("overly complex generic constant: field access is not supported in generic constants"). The block-syntax variant `{ Cfg::... .0 }` reproduces the same error.

The intended shape is documented in source via an extended comment + lint:allow rationale on the `plan_dirty` field. The lift waits on rustc gaining field-access-in-generic-consts support, tracked under `#345` (HILA-RUNTIME-C7).

Verification: `grep 'per-Cfg lift awaits rustc generic_const_exprs gaining field-access support' mock/crates/hilavitkutin/src/scheduler/mod.rs` returns one match.
