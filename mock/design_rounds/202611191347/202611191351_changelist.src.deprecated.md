# src changelist

Round topic `202611191344_topic.api-placement.md`; the doc changelist is `202611191347_changelist.doc.lock.md`. The inherited flat topic `202607200100_topic.b1-cap-lifting-completion.md` changes no source here, as the doc changelist says.

## Current state

`hilavitkutin-api` declares `capability`, `sink`, `codec` and `ceiling_div`, read by nothing but its own `tests/sink_smoke.rs` and `tests/codec_smoke.rs`; `dispatch_codegen`, read only by the engine and three bench support crates; and in `platform.rs`, below the three platform contracts, `PoolFrame`, `WakeStrategy`, `Executor`, `ExecutorError` and `HybridExecutor`, which the engine re-exports from `thread`. The api's `tests/engine_id_index.rs` tests the engine ids. On `dev`, `hilavitkutin::ui compile_fail_fixtures` is red: `unregistered_store_fail.stderr` and `unregistered_wrapped_store_fail.stderr` give line numbers three lines past where `266650fb` left the fixtures.

## Changes proposed

### 1. capability, sink, codec and ceiling_div leave the api

`src/capability.rs`, `src/sink.rs`, `src/codec.rs` and `src/ceiling_div.rs` are deleted with their `pub mod` and `pub use` lines in `src/lib.rs`, and `tests/sink_smoke.rs` and `tests/codec_smoke.rs` with them: they test only the deleted modules. Nothing is added in their place; a consumer takes notko's sink.

### 2. The codegen surface moves into the engine

`hilavitkutin-api/src/dispatch_codegen.rs` moves by `git mv` to `hilavitkutin/src/dispatch/codegen.rs`, declared `pub mod codegen` in `dispatch/mod.rs`, which re-exports every item from it. It takes the engine's seal, a new `mod sealed` in `hilavitkutin/src/lib.rs` shaped as the api's is, and bounds `StandardCodegen`'s impl on `hilavitkutin_api::RunCfg`. `StoreMarker`, which nothing read, is deleted. `dispatch/standard.rs` re-exports `StandardCodegen` from `super::codegen`. `tests/engine_id_index.rs` moves into the engine's tests with the ids.

### 3. The runtime data plane moves into the engine

`PoolFrame` and `WakeStrategy` are cut from `hilavitkutin-api/src/platform.rs` into a new `hilavitkutin/src/thread/pool_frame.rs`; `Executor`, `ExecutorError` and `HybridExecutor` into `thread/hybrid.rs`, which re-exported `HybridExecutor` before. Both take the engine's seal. `thread/mod.rs` declares `pool_frame` and re-exports `PoolFrame`, `WakeStrategy`, `Executor`, `ExecutorError` and `HybridExecutor`. `platform.rs` keeps the three contracts, the accessors and `OnePointerClosure`, and imports `PhantomData` itself.

### 4. Every reader repointed

Every engine import of a moved item names `crate::dispatch::codegen` or `crate::thread`; the engine's tests and the bench support crates `evs-support`, `wake_common` and `stc-support` name `hilavitkutin::dispatch::codegen` or `hilavitkutin::thread`. No re-export is left behind in the api.

### 5. Comments in the api

The comments of `adapt.rs`, `meta.rs`, `work_unit.rs` and `run_cfg.rs` that named `PoolFrame` or `DispatchCodegen` speak of the engine's pool frame and dispatch codegen instead.

### 6. The two ui fixtures

`tests/ui/unregistered_store_fail.stderr` and `tests/ui/unregistered_wrapped_store_fail.stderr` are regenerated by trybuild: only their line numbers change, and each still fails on the same missing `Contains<Resource<InA>>` bound. `hilavitkutin::ui compile_fail_fixtures` is green.
