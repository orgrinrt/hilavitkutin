### 2. `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, crate layout

The `hilavitkutin-api` line drops the capability, sink and codec surface and says the crate holds nothing only the engine reads, with a consumer's output going through notko's sink. The `hilavitkutin` line names what the engine declares for itself. The `hilavitkutin-persistence` line no longer says standalone: it builds on `hilavitkutin-str` and `hilavitkutin-api`, never on the engine or `hilavitkutin-ctx`.

### 3. `hilavitkutin-api/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

- The "Consumer contract surface (capability / sink / codec)" section is removed whole, and "What this crate does NOT contain" says the surface for items outside unit execution is notko's sink, a count-reporting sink belongs with the owner of the count type, and whatever only the engine reads is declared in the engine.
- "Runtime contracts" keeps what a consumer names in its own impls (`ResourceSnapshot`, `PlanAffecting`, `AccumRefused`, the lifetime contract). The `Executor`, `PoolFrame`, `WakeStrategy`, `DispatchApproach` and `CoreProgram` subsections leave it; the engine's design already describes each.
- The diagnostic coverage list drops the capability and codec lines. The store-wrapper paragraph stops pointing `Seq` and `Map` at the removed `Push`. The WorkUnit section speaks of the engine's pool frame without naming an api type for it.

### 4. `hilavitkutin-api/README.md.tmpl`

Diagnostics and byte output go through notko's sink; what only the engine reads lives in the engine.

### 5. `hilavitkutin-api/BACKLOG.md.tmpl` and `hilavitkutin/BACKLOG.md.tmpl`

The api backlog's "Sink / codec extensions, post-sweep" section is removed: its follow-ups were for a surface that leaves the crate. The three runtime follow-ups (the spin calibration bench, `Executor::try_steal_from`, the per-core-class spin ratio bench) move to the engine backlog under "Runtime data plane follow-ups". The api backlog gains "The adaptation axes as an opt-in crate", the candidate op named, with the question that decides where they go.

### 6. `hilavitkutin/DESIGN.md.tmpl`

"Dispatch codegen" says the codegen surface is the engine's, declared in `dispatch::codegen`, and describes the four ids, `CoreProgram`, `PhaseEntry`, `SyncRole`, `RecordRange`, the sealed `DispatchCodegen` and `FiberShape` and `StandardCodegen`. "Executor trait" says `Executor`, `ExecutorError` and `HybridExecutor` are declared in `thread` under the engine's own seal, with the pool frame and the wake strategy beside them.

### 7. The engine's seal is named

Added after the first src changelist was deprecated: the engine's seal needs a name of its own and a line in the design, since a second trait named as the api's `Sealed` reads to the single-source lint as one concept defined twice, and an undescribed type blocks the build. "Dispatch codegen" and "Executor trait" name it `EngineOnly`, a public trait in a private module of the engine, and say that it and the api's seal seal different things: the api's the traits a consumer names, the engine's the codegen traits and the executor.
