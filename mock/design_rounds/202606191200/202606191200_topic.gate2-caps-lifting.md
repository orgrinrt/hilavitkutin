# Topic: lift the engine grouping caps to a Caps capacity-type

**Date:** 2026-06-19
**Crate:** hilavitkutin (engine, plan/grouping.rs, scheduler/mod.rs, thread/)
**Tracked:** completion-arc P0.1b (sketch-gate) + P0.1c (Caps threading)

## Problem

The const-gated grouping walk fixes its scratch at hardcoded caps:
`GATE2_MAX_UNITS = 256` and `GATE2_MAX_ACCUMS = 16` in `plan/grouping.rs`,
`plan_dirty: [AtomicBool; 256]` and the per-(core,accum) publish array
`gate2_accum_live: [AtomicUsize; MAX_CORES * GATE2_MAX_ACCUMS]` in
`scheduler/mod.rs`, `MAX_CORES` in `thread/`. These should be
consumer-tunable, the `Capacity` associated-type pattern `PlanDims` already
uses (sketch `202606111450_cap-lifting-shapes` shape s1: GCE-free at the
consumer).

## P0.1b sketch-gate (first step in this round)

Before threading a new `Caps` trait everywhere, confirm in-engine that the
documented `GATE2_MAX_UNITS` trait-solver overflow does NOT reproduce when the
grouping scratch becomes a `<CU as ConstCapacity>::Array<_>` GAT projection
under the real compounded obligations (`BundleMasks` 4-param +
per-cons-cell `MaskProject` witnesses + `GateWith`). The reduction sketch did
not reproduce it even at depth 64; the in-engine confirm is the gate.

Key prediction (de-risks it): `BundleMasks::fill` is slice-based
(`&mut [AccessMask<CS>]`), so the per-cons-cell recursion is capacity-blind.
`CU` appears only in `masks_of`'s local scratch construction, never in the
recursive obligation, so GAT-projecting the scratch adds no per-step
`cap_size` bound. Expected: compiles, no overflow.

The first src step GAT-ifies `masks_of`'s fill scratch (sliced into `fill`
via the P0.1a `ConstCapacity::slice_mut` bridge) and `cargo check`s at the
real call sites. Outcome BRANCHES the rest:

- compiles -> lift fully: introduce a `Caps` trait
  (`type Units: Capacity`, `type Accums: Capacity`, `type PlanDirty: Capacity`,
  `MAX_CORES` if clean), thread it the way `PlanDims` threads, migrate every
  hardcoded cap site off the literals onto the GAT projections (the
  `MAX_CORES * GATE2_MAX_ACCUMS` product becomes the nested
  `Cores::Array<Accums::Array<_>>` composition per s1).
- overflows -> the grouping caps stay s1-bounded at the documented 256/16
  ceiling with the tracked lift condition; the other caps still lift.

## Out of scope

`synthesise_core_programs` / `CoreProgram` dead-weight stays (gated on
#183/P3.3 per A1-4). No behavioural change; the default caps stay 256/16/etc
so existing pipelines are byte-identical.
